## Asset-free dispositions

### `virulence-factors`

Toxins, adhesins, invasins, capsules, biofilms, metal acquisition, and many unrelated enzymes are combined under a class heading. It remains asset-free pending one exact factor, organism, molecular state, compartment, host target, and evidence record.

### `vitamin-b12`

Cyanocobalamin, methylcobalamin, adenosylcobalamin, and other cobalamin states are not interchangeable molecules or cofactor contexts. It remains asset-free pending an exact controlled cobalamin form and cobalt/ligand/state specification.

### `vitamin-d`

Vitamin D2, D3, precursors, circulating metabolites, and active calcitriol are conflated with exposure, supplementation, status, receptor signaling, and clinical associations. It remains asset-free pending exact molecule-, specimen-, assay-, and endpoint-specific records.

### `zinc-metalloprotease`

Host and bacterial enzyme families and incompatible catalytic descriptions are merged. It remains asset-free pending a corrected, accession-specific enzyme and active-state record.

### `zonulin`

Pre-haptoglobin-2 identity, multiple HP transcripts/products, intestinal physiology, serum measurement, and commercial ELISA signal are treated as one analyte despite documented assay cross-reactivity. It remains asset-free pending separate sequence-specific protein and assay-specific biomarker records.
