## Asset-free dispositions

### `infant-exposure`

This umbrella route collapses heterogeneous prenatal, dietary, environmental, and iatrogenic exposures into a single developmental picture and includes medically unsafe prenatal-chelation framing. It remains asset-free until age, route, analyte, product, endpoint, regulatory scope, and evidence level are separated and treatment language is corrected.

### `insulin-resistance`

The controlled physiological state is dominated by PCOS and metal narratives, uncertain chromium biology, causal extrapolation, and intervention implications. It remains asset-free until operational measurement, tissue and syndrome context, evidence tiers, and guideline-grounded care are explicit.

### `interleukin-6`

Human IL-6 is exactly identifiable, but its classic, trans-, and cluster signaling are simplified into universal protective and pathological arms and then connected to broad metal, disease, biomarker, supplementation, and treatment claims. It remains asset-free until exact receptor and cell contexts and evidence boundaries are rebuilt.

### `intestinal-permeability`

The physiological process is presented as a universal disease gateway while commercial serum zonulin and causal and therapeutic conclusions exceed measurement evidence. It remains asset-free until pathway, site, probe, assay, barrier-injury distinction, and clinical-validation status are explicit.

### `iron-sulfur-clusters`

Diverse clusters, proteins, organisms, compartments, and assembly systems are collapsed into a universal toxic-metal target with unsupported organism dependencies. It remains asset-free until a defined cluster/protein/system scope and exposure-specific evidence replace the universal mechanism.

### `irving-williams-series`

A conditional coordination-chemistry trend is presented as exceptionless across ligands, proteins, oxidation states, cells, toxicants, and diseases. It remains asset-free until thermodynamics, kinetics, cellular availability, transport, geometry, and non-series ions are separated.
