## Asset-free dispositions

### `horizontal-gene-transfer`

The page conflates the MeSH-defined transfer process with mobile genetic elements, treats integrons as independently mobile, and universalizes co-selection, `intI1`, metal persistence, diet, and biofilm claims. It remains asset-free until those identities and evidence levels are reconciled.

### `hmos`

The HMO family lacks one exact controlled identifier. The page's “sole purpose,” universal digestibility, formula-absence, Bifidobacterium/Proteobacteria, and NEC claims conflict with current reviews, clinical evidence boundaries, and current multi-HMO formula use. It remains asset-free until structure-, product-, strain-, model-, and population-specific scope is restored.

### `hydrogen-sulfide`

The page collapses neutral H2S, HS−, total sulfide, and donor experiments; underrepresents cysteine and other microbial production routes; and overextends protocol, diet/metal, disease, and metal-precipitation evidence. It remains asset-free until chemical species, dose, tissue, assay, and model boundaries are explicit.

### `hydrogenase`

The generic page is not exactly identified by the ferredoxin-hydrogenase MeSH record and compresses a diverse enzyme family into a pathogen-focused [NiFe] narrative. Malformed `.md` source keys and unsupported host-substrate, saturation, nickel, virulence, cancer, diet, and therapeutic claims prevent trustworthy provenance. It remains asset-free pending scope and source repair.

### `hyperaccumulator`

The page replaces element-specific botanical criteria with a 10–100× comparison and misclassifies ordinary foods/crops as hyperaccumulator species, then asserts an unsupported food-to-disease chain. It remains asset-free until true hyperaccumulators, contaminant accumulation, phytoextraction, and dietary exposure are separated.

### `hyperandrogenism`

The page's central metal mechanism is chemically false: reviewed human CYP19A1 aromatase uses a heme-iron cofactor, not zinc. The page also stores the 2025 trace-elements paper under an unrelated DOI and converts heterogeneous, mostly observational PCOS microbiome evidence into central causation and treatment language. It remains asset-free until the false mechanism, source metadata, condition scope, and evidence grading are corrected.
