## Asset-free dispositions

### `metallothionein`

The family is reduced to one fixed mammalian structure, stoichiometry, affinity hierarchy, and disease story without a selected organism, isoform, metalation state, or experimental condition. It remains asset-free pending an accession- or structure-specific treatment.

### `methylation`

Distinct DNA, histone, protein, and xenobiotic processes are combined with one-carbon metabolism and strong microbiome/metal causality. It remains asset-free pending process-, substrate-, enzyme-, tissue-, assay-, and evidence-specific reconstruction.

### `microbial-biomarkers`

Associations, pathogen tests, host proteins, metabolites, and experimental metallophores are presented as clinical biomarkers without defined contexts of use or analytical and clinical validation. It remains asset-free pending a standards-based biomarker framework.

### `metabolites`

The route duplicates `microbiome-derived-metabolites` and merges host, dietary, microbial, and co-metabolic origins with categorical beneficial/toxic labels. It remains asset-free pending canonical reconciliation and compound-specific attribution and validation.

### `microbiome-diversity-metrics`

Metrics with different assumptions are presented as interchangeable disease findings and generic beta-diversity significance is implied without design, preprocessing, dispersion, or effect-size boundaries. It remains asset-free pending a mathematically explicit methods treatment.

### `microbiome-derived-metabolites`

The route duplicates `metabolites` while making stronger microbial-origin and disease-causality claims across heterogeneous compounds and models. It remains asset-free pending route reconciliation and molecule-, pathway-, compartment-, dose-, model-, and human-evidence boundaries.
