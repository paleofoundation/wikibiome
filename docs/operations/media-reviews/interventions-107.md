## Review method

The deterministic six-route intervention queue was checked against the current generated article corpus and current NLM MeSH records. Review tested controlled identity, whether a reproducible product or diet protocol was selected, whether the local evidence fixed one observable physical or measured state, and whether a narrow image could be published without becoming invented treatment guidance.

All six routes fail the pre-generation gate. No marked prompt exists and the built-in image generator was not called. There is consequently no source image, hash, crop, visual claim, master, derivative, or metadata-bearing candidate to review. This is an intentional fail-closed outcome, not a stalled batch.

## Asset-free dispositions

### `lactoferrin-supplementation`

MeSH D007781 identifies lactoferrin as a protein, not one supplement. The zero-source route does not select biological source, sequence or product, glycosylation, iron loading, processing, formulation, dose, population, assay, or endpoint. A protein model or commercial-looking product would invent the missing state. The route remains asset-free until one exact product or molecular state and direct evidence are selected.

### `low-carbohydrate-diet-gerd`

MeSH D050528 identifies a broad carbohydrate-restricted diet class, not a universal GERD protocol. The zero-source route does not fix carbohydrate amount or quality, energy balance, foods, duration, population, medication context, adherence, reflux assay, or endpoint. A plated meal would silently prescribe an unsupported implementation. The route remains asset-free until one measured protocol is selected.

### `low-carbohydrate-diet`

MeSH D050528 again identifies only the broad diet class. The zero-source local page combines multiple carbohydrate thresholds, PCOS and GERD contexts, energy and weight states, foods, supplements, biomarkers, mechanisms, and outcomes. It remains asset-free until one PCOS-specific protocol and measured state are selected.

### `low-fat-diet`

MeSH D018752 supplies a general fat-restricted diet definition but does not validate an endometriosis protocol. The zero-source page combines fat classes, substitutions, energy balance, hormones, bile acids, inflammation, microbiota, symptoms, and nutrient cautions. A food image would invent the protocol and imply treatment guidance. The route remains asset-free until one controlled diet and outcome are selected.

### `low-nickel-diet`

MeSH D009532 identifies elemental nickel, not a low-nickel diet. Although this route has 29 linked sources, they span different food-composition datasets, analytical methods and units, preparations, thresholds, diagnoses, sensitization phenotypes, challenge designs, co-interventions, durations, and outcomes. A universal allowed/restricted-food image would be scientifically unstable and prescriptive. The route remains asset-free until a jurisdictional dataset, complete protocol, measured exposure state, population, and endpoint are fixed.

### `low-red-meat-diet`

MeSH D000069466 defines the red-meat food class, not a low-red-meat endometriosis intervention. The zero-source route does not fix baseline or target intake, species and cuts, processing, preparation, substitutions, energy balance, duration, population, adherence, assay, or endpoint. It remains asset-free until one complete restriction-and-substitution protocol is selected.
