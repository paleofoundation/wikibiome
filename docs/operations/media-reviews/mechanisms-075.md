## Asset-free dispositions

### `hyperparathyroidism`

The general condition is reframed as a causal metal-driven secondary pathway and chelation hypothesis from insufficient evidence. It remains asset-free until primary, secondary, and tertiary disease are separated; the association source and units are verified; and causal and treatment extrapolations are removed in favor of guideline-bounded clinical scope.

### `hypoxia`

The page applies a universal oxygen threshold while conflating physiologic intestinal hypoxia with inflammatory and tumour settings. Incorrect organism physiology, an incorrect urease function, unsupported ecological claims, and broken cross-references prevent trustworthy media context. It remains asset-free pending context-specific terminology and source repair.

### `hypoxic-signaling`

A broad HIF pathway is reduced to nickel carcinogenesis, HIF-1 is conflated with HIF-1α, and the prolyl-hydroxylation description is chemically incoherent. It remains asset-free until identity, normoxic regulation, metal-induced signaling, hydroxylation chemistry, and evidence grades are reconciled.

### `il-10`

It remains asset-free until protein, receptor, cell, compartment, cohort, model, and treatment boundaries are explicit.

### `il-17`

It remains asset-free until ligand, family, species, cell-source, tissue, and clinical contexts are separated.

It remains asset-free until precursor and processing routes are defined and material, form, dose, cell, assay, and disease evidence are bounded.
