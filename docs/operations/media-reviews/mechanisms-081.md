## Asset-free dispositions

It remains asset-free pending an IUPAC-consistent, element- and method-specific reconstruction.

### `metallochaperone`

Diverse organism-, metal-, protein-, compartment-, and energy-specific systems are reduced to one universal escort and direct-handoff mechanism. It remains asset-free pending protein/client identifiers, structures, stoichiometry, assays, and demonstrated transfer boundaries.

### `metalloestrogen`

The singular route duplicates the plural concept while making receptor and disease-causality claims stronger than the cited human evidence. It remains asset-free pending canonical identity reconciliation and species-, receptor-, assay-, dose-, model-, and outcome-specific evidence grading.

### `metalloestrogens`

Heterogeneous ions, chemical species, assays, receptors, doses, tissues, and observational findings are presented as one causal endocrine-disrupting class despite conflicting population evidence. It remains asset-free pending chemical- and endpoint-specific reconstruction and duplicate-route resolution.

### `metallomics`

Multiple analytical disciplines and heterogeneous discovery cohorts are converted into universal disease patterns and diagnostic tiers without adequate preanalytics, QC, interference, validation, or intended-use boundaries. It remains asset-free pending a standards-centered analytical and validation framework.

### `metalloregulator`

Multiple protein families and organism-specific examples become universal coordination, network, virulence, co-selection, and therapeutic rules. It remains asset-free pending regulator-, organism-, metal-, regulon-, assay-, and phenotype-specific reconstruction.
