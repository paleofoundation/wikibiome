## Review method

The deterministic six-route intervention queue was checked against the current generated article corpus, its source resolver, and current NLM MeSH identity records. Review tested whether each route selected an exact ingredient or microorganism identity, a reproducible product composition, a bounded clinical protocol, and a visually honest non-promotional state supported by direct evidence.

All six routes fail the pre-generation gate. No marker-bound generation prompt exists and the built-in image generator was not called. There is consequently no generated source, hash, crop, visual claim, master, derivative, or metadata-bearing candidate to review. This is an intentional fail-closed disposition, not a stalled batch.

## Asset-free dispositions

### `prebiotics-inulin-fructo-oligosaccharide`

MeSH D056692 identifies a broad prebiotic class, D007444 identifies inulin, D005630 identifies fructans, and D011085 identifies PCOS. The zero-source page does not specify the molecular distribution, chain length, linkage profile, source, processing, inulin-to-FOS ratio, purity, formulation, dose, or measured protocol. A powder, plant, polymer, or ovary image would invent the missing state and could imply unsupported treatment guidance.

### `probiotic-combination-ckd`

MeSH D019936 identifies probiotics as a class and D051436 identifies chronic kidney insufficiency. The zero-source route lists three organism labels but no strain accessions, viable-count allocation, product, lot, excipients, storage, CKD stage, dose, comparator, or measured endpoint. A capsule or mixed-bacteria image would imply a product composition the record does not establish.

### `probiotics-general`

The sole resolved source concerns the genomic classification of strain 35624. The page itself spans many bacterial and yeast taxa, single- and multi-strain products, disease populations, doses, mechanisms, efficacy claims, and safety states. MeSH D019936 does not make one of these a representative visual identity. The route requires an explicit class-level visual-governance plan or a narrower product scope.

### `probiotics-lactobacillus-bifidobacterium`

The zero-source PCOS route lists four organism labels but no current strain accessions, per-strain counts, product, formulation, storage, dose, duration, or outcome protocol. A mixed morphology plate would falsely convert genus or species labels into a verified intervention composition.

### `probiotics-asd-dysbiosis`

Every one of the five declared local source identifiers retains an erroneous `.md` suffix and fails the normal source resolver, although similarly named source records exist without those suffixes. The route also spans multiple incompletely identified taxa, formulations, doses, heterogeneous outcomes, and speculative mechanisms. No single capsule, gut, brain, child, or bacterial-community image can represent that scope without inventing protocol details or risking stigmatizing imagery.

### `probiotics-thyroid`

The zero-source Hashimoto route provides only broad Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium labels. MeSH D050031 identifies Hashimoto disease, but the page does not establish exact strains, composition, product, storage, dose, duration, disease state, comparator, assay, endpoint, or safety boundary. A thyroid-plus-bacteria montage would be decorative rather than scientifically verifiable.
