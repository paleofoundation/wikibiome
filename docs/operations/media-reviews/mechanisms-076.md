## Asset-free dispositions

### `il-8`

Human CXCL8 is exactly identifiable, but the article reduces it to one gingipain narrative and unbounded disease associations. It does not establish the experimentally resolved protein assembly or tissue context that an accurate image would represent. The route remains asset-free pending identity-centered article repair and a defensible representation boundary.

### `immune-balance`

This umbrella term is not one controlled biological entity. The page presents heterogeneous, plastic immune states as a binary equilibrium and assigns broad metal, microbiome, disease, and treatment effects as though they were one mechanism. It remains asset-free until a measurable scope and evidence boundaries replace the simplified system.

### `immunotherapy`

The broad MeSH class includes more than cancer checkpoint inhibition, while the article makes checkpoint therapy its near-exclusive scope and turns cohort-dependent microbiome associations and experimental interventions into clinical rules. It remains asset-free until taxonomy, regimen, evidence tier, and treatment status are reconciled.

### `indole`

The exact 1H-indole molecule is mixed with multiple derivative structures and context-dependent receptor, concentration, species, and disease claims. It also overlaps materially with the separate `indoles` route. It remains asset-free pending parent/derivative separation and duplicate-route reconciliation.

### `indoles`

The broad chemical class is redefined as an exclusively microbial pathway, and compounds with different structures, producers, host transformations, receptors, concentrations, and effects are collapsed into one protective axis. It remains asset-free until class or pathway scope and compound-specific evidence are explicit.

### `indoxyl-sulfate`

The exact metabolite and core biosynthetic sequence are identifiable, but the page universalizes producers, cadmium causation, organ damage, BBB passage, biomarker status, and treatment implications from mixed model and association evidence. It remains asset-free pending transporter-direction, species, model, association, and clinical-evidence repair.
