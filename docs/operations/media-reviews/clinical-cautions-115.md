## Asset-free dispositions

### `stop-formula-feeding-without-supplementation-nec`

This zero-source route does not define gestational age, birth weight, maternal or donor milk availability, preterm formula, fortifier, probiotic, feeding schedule, NICU protocol, growth needs, comparator, or NEC outcome. Current guidance favors human milk while preserving conditional formula use and fortification needs. A bottle, breast, infant bowel, formula powder, supplement, or prohibition sign could be interpreted as feeding advice and would erase those contingencies. The route remains asset-free.

### `stop-high-protein-keto-diet-ckd`

The route conflates two distinct controlled diet concepts and does not define macronutrient composition, energy intake, protein source, CKD stage, albuminuria, dialysis, diabetes, nutritional status, duration, comparator, or outcomes. Protein requirements vary across CKD states, including increased needs during dialysis. Kidney, ketone, meat, food-plate, or crossed-out-meal imagery would imply a universal restriction that the route has not established. The route remains asset-free.

### `stop-iodine-supplementation-graves`

The route does not identify iodine compound, product, dose, baseline iodine status, pregnancy, thyroid state, concurrent therapy, population, comparator, monitoring, or outcome. Iodine is essential, excess and deficiency are not equivalent, and susceptibility varies. Thyroid, salt, seaweed, crystal, tablet, or prohibition imagery would be prescriptive while failing to represent the actual intervention state. The route remains asset-free.

### `stop-iodine-supplementation-hashimotos`

Supplement form, elemental dose, dietary exposure, deficiency, pregnancy, thyroid function, autoantibodies, levothyroxine use, population, monitoring, and outcomes are unresolved. Autoimmune-thyroid susceptibility does not make all iodine sources or clinical states interchangeable. Generic thyroid or supplement imagery would duplicate the reviewed disease asset and convert a conditional boundary into universal advice. The route remains asset-free.

### `stop-iron-supplementation-ibd`

The route does not resolve iron-deficiency or anemia status, IBD phenotype and activity, bleeding, absorption, oral versus intravenous route, ferrous versus ferric formulation, elemental dose, tolerance, monitoring, or outcomes. Current guidance supports iron repletion and selects route based on inflammation, absorption, response, and tolerance rather than recommending a categorical stop. Bowel, blood, tablet, infusion, iron-atom, or prohibition imagery could imply withholding needed treatment. The reviewed elemental Iron image is not a supplement and cannot substitute for an exact clinical product. The route remains asset-free.

### `stop-iron-supplementation-alzheimers`

The linked evidence is mechanistic or associative and does not establish a universal clinical supplementation rule. Deficiency, anemia, overload, systemic and brain biomarkers, compound, route, elemental dose, Alzheimer stage, population, comparator, monitoring, cognition, and safety outcomes are unresolved. Brain, plaque, iron, pill, infusion, or crossed-out-product imagery would convert association into advice. Existing reviewed Alzheimer disease and elemental Iron images are not evidence for this combined claim. The route remains asset-free.
