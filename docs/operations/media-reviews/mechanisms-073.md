## Review method

The deterministic queue contained four routes that failed the pre-generation scientific and editorial gate and two amino-acid routes cleared for narrow heavy-atom-connectivity plates. The held routes received no prompt, no generation call, and no raster. `histidine` and `homocysteine` each received exactly one built-in image-generation call from its marked immutable prompt; no retry or image edit was attempted.

Both returned generator-owned PNGs were copied byte-identically into the project. Control Tower and independent reviewers inspected them at native 1536 × 1024 detail and verified their exact SHA-256 values. Prompt intent was treated as untrusted. Review counted literal visible centers and connectors, reconstructed the displayed graph, inspected prohibited content and alpha occupancy, measured the outer 32-pixel band and alpha-defined subject bounds, and compared those bounds with the centered 1100 × 530 safety box and 1200 × 630 social crop.

No generated output is eligible for exact-hash acceptance or promotion. Histidine fails chemical identity, count, background, and crop gates. Homocysteine passes heavy-atom count and adjacency but independently fails the opaque-field and crop gates. Both are immutable non-public quarantines.
