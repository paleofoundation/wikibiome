## Review method

The deterministic five-route end-of-group queue was checked against the current generated article corpus and current NLM MeSH, UniProt, and enzyme-nomenclature records. Review tested exact identity, chemical or molecular state, organism and experimental scope, whether article claims select one observable subject, and whether a narrow representation could be published without inheriting false universality.

All five routes fail the pre-generation gate. No marked prompt exists and the built-in image generator was not called. There is consequently no source image, hash, crop, visual claim, master, derivative, or metadata-bearing candidate to review. This is an intentional fail-closed outcome, not a stalled batch.

## Asset-free dispositions

### `siderophore-competition`

MeSH D017262 controls the siderophore chemical-function class, but competition is an ecological process involving exact producers, ligands, metal states, receptors, media, concentrations, and outcomes. The article spans numerous organisms, compounds, host countermeasures, infection contexts, and interventions. It remains asset-free until one experimentally bounded competition state is selected.

### `siderophores`

MeSH D017262 encompasses chemically distinct siderophores and does not define one canonical molecular graph. The article adds multiple producers, ferric complexes, receptors, diagnostics, and antimicrobial applications. It remains asset-free until one compound, stereochemical and protonation state, producer, metal occupancy, structure source, compartment, assay, and claim boundary are fixed.

### `siderophores-metallophores`

This umbrella route spans multiple chelator families and multiple metals, oxidation states, occupancies, transport systems, defensive functions, and applications. A generic multicolor chelation image would imply unsupported binding and transport. It remains asset-free until one named compound-metal-organism state and measured endpoint are fixed.

### `superoxide-dismutase`

MeSH D013482 and EC 1.15.1.1 identify an enzyme class, but the article combines non-equivalent human and microbial proteins, cofactors, oligomers, cellular compartments, metallation states, infection phenotypes, and biomarker claims. It remains asset-free until one species, sequence, isoform, cofactor state, experimental structure, compartment, and assay are selected.

### `transferrin`

Human serotransferrin is identifiable as UniProt P02787 and MeSH D014168, but the article combines apo, monoferric, and diferric protein, glycoforms, receptors, pH-dependent release, serum assays, other-metal binding, infection, and disease contexts. A state-specific structure cannot stand for all of these. It remains asset-free until exact molecular, iron-loading, structural, specimen, and assay fields are fixed.
