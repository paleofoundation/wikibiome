## Authority and representation boundary

- Polycystic Ovary Syndrome is NLM MeSH `D011085`; MeSH explicitly states that the name is misleading and describes atretic follicles rather than true cysts. The plate therefore prohibits ovarian cyst shorthand and any follicle-count or phenotype claim.
- Depression, Postpartum is NLM MeSH `D019052`. NIMH describes postpartum depression as part of perinatal depression, a heterogeneous treatable mood disorder without one cause. The plate uses neutral brain, postpartum anatomy, and time orientation without a patient, emotion stereotype, mechanism, or diagnostic sign.
- Primary Ovarian Insufficiency is the current NLM MeSH heading `D016649` for the canonical premature-ovarian-insufficiency route. MeSH permits the condition with or without follicle depletion, and NICHD distinguishes it from premature menopause. The plate therefore prohibits an empty-ovary or irreversible-failure metaphor.
- Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder is NLM MeSH `D065446`. HHS Office on Women's Health describes a recurring premenstrual timing relationship but notes that its cause is not known for certain. The plate therefore represents timing without symptom caricature, hormone level, or mechanism.
- Psoriasis is NLM MeSH `D011565`. NIAMS describes multiple forms and variable sites, with scaly inflamed skin as a common feature. The plate uses one restrained local surface change and a neutral nail unit without asserting subtype, distribution, skin tone, or diagnosis.
- Arthritis, Rheumatoid is NLM MeSH `D001172`. NIAMS describes a systemic autoimmune disease that primarily affects synovial joints and may affect many sites. The plate uses a hand orientation and bounded synovial change without deformation, erosion, stage, or universal distribution.
