- Identity: NLM MeSH `D007113` identifies Immunity, Innate as an organism-level capacity involving naturally occurring anti-infective agents, constitutional factors, and immediately acting immune cells. Related MeSH `D000094406` separately identifies innate-immunity recognition systems.
- Scope conflict: the article combines physical barriers, mucus, antimicrobial peptides and proteins, TLR and NOD recognition, macrophages, neutrophils, dendritic cells, complement-like soluble defenses, nutritional immunity, phagosome copper and zinc handling, intestinal ecology, Crohn's disease, and cancer. These are different subjects at organism, tissue, cell, molecular, and pathway scales.
- Representation risk: a single cell, receptor, pathway, or anatomical barrier would falsely imply that it is the canonical visual identity of innate immunity. A montage would be a designed taxonomy rather than an observed specimen.
- Required resolution: select one organism, tissue and compartment, cell type, receptor or effector, activation state, stimulus, time point, specimen or assay, endpoint, and evidence tier.
- Authorities: https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D007113 ; https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D000094406
