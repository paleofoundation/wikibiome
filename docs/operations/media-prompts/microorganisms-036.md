## Identity and morphology evidence

- `sphingobium`: current genus `NCBITaxon:165695`; type species *Sphingobium yanoikuyae*. NCBI Taxonomy and LPSN preserve the genus erected by Takeuchi et al. (2001); BacDive type-strain evidence supports Gram-negative rod morphology. The reconstruction omits appendages rather than making a genus-wide motility claim.
- `sphingomonas`: current genus `NCBITaxon:13687`; type species *Sphingomonas paucimobilis*. NCBI Taxonomy, LPSN, and BacDive type-strain evidence support short rod morphology. Cellular shape is not diagnostic across the large genus.
- `staphylococcus`: current conserved genus `NCBITaxon:1279`; type species *Staphylococcus aureus*. NCBI Taxonomy and LPSN support the name and type; NCBI Medical Microbiology supports spherical cocci occurring chiefly in irregular clusters, with pairs and short chains also possible.
- `staphylococcus-aureus`: current species `NCBITaxon:1280`; type strain ATCC 12600 / DSM 20231. NCBI Taxonomy and NCBI Bookshelf support spherical cocci with a strong tendency toward grape-like clusters. Golden colony pigmentation is deliberately not depicted at the cell level.
- `staphylococcus-epidermidis`: current species `NCBITaxon:1282`; type strain ATCC 14990 / DSM 20044. NCBI Taxonomy and NCBI Bookshelf support Gram-positive cocci in clusters. Shape alone cannot distinguish the species from other staphylococci.
- `streptococcus`: current genus `NCBITaxon:1301`; type species *Streptococcus pyogenes*. NCBI Taxonomy and LPSN support the name and type; NCBI Medical Microbiology supports round-to-ovoid cocci in pairs or chains formed by division in one plane.
