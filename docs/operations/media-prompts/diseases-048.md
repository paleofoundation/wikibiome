## Authority and representation boundary

- NLM MeSH 2026 descriptors: Renal Insufficiency, Chronic `D051436`; Liver Cirrhosis `D008103`; Colorectal Neoplasms `D015179`; Coronary Artery Disease `D003324`; Crohn Disease `D003424`; Cystic Fibrosis `D003550`.
- Chronic kidney disease is defined by reduced kidney function persisting for more than three months and spans multiple stages and causes. The plate therefore avoids a universal gross kidney lesion and confines representative change to a separate microanatomy model.
- Liver cirrhosis involves variable destruction and alteration of hepatic architecture, with fibrous septa surrounding regenerative nodules. The limited cutaway communicates that architecture without asserting etiology or stage.
- Colorectal neoplasms arise in the colon or rectum, and NCI notes that many begin as lesions of the inner surface. The plate shows one localized mucosal lesion without staging, spread, or obstruction.
- Coronary artery disease includes congenital, atherosclerotic, and non-atherosclerotic processes. Because plaque is common but not universal, the heart remains neutral and plaque appears only in a separate representative artery cross-section.
- Crohn disease may affect any part of the digestive tract and is characteristically segmental and transmural. The plate limits its claim to a separate representative wall model and does not assert a universal distribution or complication.
- Cystic fibrosis is an exocrine-gland disorder expressed in multiple organs including lungs and pancreas. The plate preserves that multisystem scope and does not reduce the disease to infection or one pulmonary appearance.
