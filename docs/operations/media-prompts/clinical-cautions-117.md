## Route audits

### `stop-iron-supplementation-long-covid`

Disease phenotype and activity, disability, concurrent therapy, inflammation, deficiency, anemia, route, formulation, elemental dose, monitoring, neurologic outcomes, and safety remain unresolved. Brain, spinal cord, myelin, blood, or product imagery would hide those clinical variables.

### `stop-iron-supplementation-obesity`

MeSH D009765 identifies obesity but not the route's proposed functional-anemia phenotype. Inflammation, absolute versus functional deficiency, anemia, bleeding, malabsorption, bariatric surgery, pregnancy, formulation, route, dose, and outcomes remain unresolved. Patient or body-shape imagery would be stigmatizing, while product or elemental imagery would be prescriptive.

### `stop-iron-supplementation-parkinsons`

MeSH D010300 identifies Parkinson disease but not stage, treatment, or anemia state. Menstrual blood loss, pregnancy or fertility treatment, deficiency, anemia, inflammation, formulation, route, elemental dose, metabolic and reproductive outcomes, and safety remain unresolved. Ovary, follicle, body-shape, blood, or product imagery would be scientifically misleading or stigmatizing.
