## Authority and representation boundary

- NLM MeSH `D006639` supplies the article-level biomedical descriptor for histidine. Heavy-atom adjacency is abstracted from ChEBI `CHEBI:27570`, but that record also encodes a neutral prototropic form, formal bond orders, and net charge and is therefore not an exact identifier for the deliberately protonation-, tautomer-, bond-order-, stereochemistry-, and conformation-agnostic plate. The plate also does not claim stereospecific L-histidine (`CHEBI:15971`; PubChem CID 6274) or L-histidine zwitterion (`CHEBI:57595`).
- NLM MeSH `D006710` supplies the article-level biomedical descriptor for homocysteine and explicitly warns against confusion with homocystine. Heavy-atom adjacency is abstracted from ChEBI `CHEBI:17230`, but that record also encodes a neutral prototropic form, formal bond orders, and net charge and is therefore not an exact identifier for the deliberately protonation-, bond-order-, stereochemistry-, and conformation-agnostic plate. The plate also does not claim stereospecific L-homocysteine (`CHEBI:17588`; PubChem CID 91552), L-homocysteine zwitterion (`CHEBI:58199`; PubChem CID 6971015), or total plasma homocysteine.
- Histidine has exactly 11 heavy atoms and an 11-link adjacency graph at this deliberately bond-order-agnostic level: 6 carbon, 3 nitrogen, and 2 oxygen centers; its five-membered imidazole ring has 3 carbon and 2 nitrogen centers.
- Homocysteine has exactly 8 heavy atoms and a 7-link open adjacency graph at this deliberately bond-order-agnostic level: 4 carbon, 1 nitrogen, 2 oxygen, and 1 sulfur center. A second sulfur or sulfur–sulfur link would depict homocystine and must fail review.
- The current histidine article should separately be corrected from “semi-essential” to essential/indispensable for humans and should narrow its nickel-allergy, *Proteus mirabilis* HypB, and oxidative-stress language. The current homocysteine article should distinguish association from causation, free monomer from total plasma homocysteine, and observational microbiome correlations from demonstrated control. Those editorial corrections remain outside this media-only batch; the two plates make no corresponding biological or clinical claim.
