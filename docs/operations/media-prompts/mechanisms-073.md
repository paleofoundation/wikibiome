- Histidine has exactly 11 heavy atoms and an 11-link adjacency graph at this deliberately bond-order-agnostic level: 6 carbon, 3 nitrogen, and 2 oxygen centers; its five-membered imidazole ring has 3 carbon and 2 nitrogen centers.
- Homocysteine has exactly 8 heavy atoms and a 7-link open adjacency graph at this deliberately bond-order-agnostic level: 4 carbon, 1 nitrogen, 2 oxygen, and 1 sulfur center. A second sulfur or sulfur–sulfur link would depict homocystine and must fail review.
- The current histidine article should separately be corrected from “semi-essential” to essential/indispensable for humans and should narrow its nickel-allergy, *Proteus mirabilis* HypB, and oxidative-stress language. The current homocysteine article should distinguish association from causation, free monomer from total plasma homocysteine, and observational microbiome correlations from demonstrated control. Those editorial corrections remain outside this media-only batch; the two plates make no corresponding biological or clinical claim.
