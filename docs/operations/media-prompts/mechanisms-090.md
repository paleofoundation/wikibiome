## Route audits

### `propionic-acid`

Propionic acid is chemically identifiable as propanoic acid, PubChem CID 1032, but the page turns one cross-sectional constipated-ASD cohort and preclinical intracerebroventricular models into a general human causal narrative. It asserts a universal healthy/pathological concentration threshold, a decisive SCFA ratio, Bacteroidetes enrichment, diet causation, citrate-synthase inhibition, metal synergy, and therapeutic prescriptions without matched matrices, units, exposures, models, or evidence tiers. Separate the chemical record from propionate salts, physiological compartments, microbial pathways, analytical measurements, animal dosing models, human associations, and interventions.

Authorities: https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/1032 ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/propionic-acid

### `quinolinic-acid`

Quinolinic acid is chemically identifiable as pyridine-2,3-dicarboxylic acid, PubChem CID 1066, and can act as an NMDA-receptor agonist. The page nevertheless collapses cellular kynurenine branches, peripheral and central compartments, cytokine signaling, microbiome influence, serotonin competition, measured concentrations, and associations across several disorders into a single dysbiosis-to-neurotoxicity chain. It needs exact metabolite/species, compartment, producing cell and enzymes, concentration and assay, model, receptor/endpoint, disease cohort, and evidence boundaries before a primary image can carry more than a disconnected chemical identity.

Authorities: https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Quinolinic-Acid ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8973572/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24089628/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/quinolinic-acid

### `quorum-sensing`

The route merges multiple signal families, synthases, receptors, organisms, population geometries, environmental contexts, phenotypes, interkingdom interactions, and inhibition strategies. AHL systems cannot stand in for peptide, AI-2, indole, or fungal signaling, and cell density alone does not define every circuit. Claims that quorum quenching avoids resistance selection and that quorum sensing universally switches biofilm, virulence, siderophore, and resistance programs require system-specific evidence. Split by named signal–synthase–receptor–organism circuit, concentration/diffusion context, regulated target, assay, phenotype, and intervention.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31612240/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41627464/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/quorum-sensing

### `riboswitch`

The page treats the diverse riboswitch field as a metal-sensing mechanism, says every riboswitch has one architecture, and generalizes location, co-transcriptional kinetics, speed, reversibility, ligand coordination, and regulatory output. It then merges Mn-sensing yybP-ykoY, NiCo, Mg, pH-responsive alx, and Rho-dependent control across different organisms. Split by riboswitch class and exact sequence/organism, genomic context, ligand and coordination evidence, structure accession, expression platform, kinetic/thermodynamic regime, assay, regulated gene, and phenotype.

Authorities: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5473149/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5465474/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19298181/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/riboswitch

### `saccharolytic-fermentation`

The page reduces diverse carbohydrate fermentation networks to a universally beneficial proximal-colon mode and protein fermentation to a universally toxic distal-colon mode. Substrates, organisms, cross-feeding, gases, short-chain fatty acids, branched-chain products, spatial gradients, diets, models, and health outcomes are merged; methane is not a direct bacterial carbohydrate-fermentation product in the same sense as the listed acids, and several protein-derived metabolites are context dependent. Split by substrate–organism–pathway–product module with location, flux or concentration, assay, diet, host/model, comparator, and endpoint.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22121108/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29298101/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9120404/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/saccharolytic-fermentation

### `semen-microbiome`

Semen is a low-microbial-biomass, high-host-background sample in which collection, urethral/skin carryover, reagents, sequencing depth, viability, taxonomic classifier, sexual and clinical covariates, and negative controls materially affect results. The page presents core genera, dysbiosis, metal selection, sperm effects, and probiotic promise more firmly than the current evidence supports and does not distinguish semen, seminal plasma, sperm-associated material, or male-tract sites. Rebuild around specimen definition, collection and abstinence protocol, negative controls, extraction, 16S region or shotgun workflow, host depletion, absolute load, viability, cohort, fertility phenotype, covariates, and replicated evidence.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32794312/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12176750/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13407389/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/semen-microbiome
