## Authority and representation boundary

- NLM MeSH `D012336` identifies prokaryotic 16S ribosomal RNA. NCBI's Human Microbiome Program methods overview describes 16S rRNA sequencing as a way to ask which organisms are present, while NCBI's clinical sequencing review cautions that closely related organisms may not be resolved. The plate therefore avoids literal sequences, species-level promises, functional inference, and quantitative output.
- PubChem CID `175` and ChEBI `30089` identify acetate anion; PubChem CID `176` is the distinct parent acid, acetic acid. The plate therefore uses acetate as the controlled identity and does not silently substitute the acid.
- `acidic-microenvironment` remains a WikiBiome-controlled ecological concept because local extracellular acidity is context-dependent and must not be conflated with systemic acidosis or a single disease ontology. The plate uses only a qualitative localized field and claims no pH value.
- UniProtKB `P35869` identifies reviewed human aryl hydrocarbon receptor. UniProt describes a ligand-activated transcription factor that can translocate and act with ARNT, but the plate avoids a literal protein structure, binding pose, activation state, or causal pathway.

- UniProtKB `Q9UEF7` identifies human Klotho. The plate uses generic membrane/soluble protein context and renal anatomy without a literal structure, cleavage event, expression level, or anti-aging claim.
