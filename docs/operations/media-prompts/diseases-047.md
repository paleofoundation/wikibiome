## Authority and representation boundary

- NLM MeSH 2026 descriptors: Barrett Esophagus `D001471`; Breast Neoplasms `D001943`; Celiac Disease `D002446`; Cerebral Palsy `D002547`; Cholestasis `D002779`; Fatigue Syndrome, Chronic `D015673`.
- MeSH defines Celiac Disease through small-intestinal inflammation and loss of microvilli structure, supporting a representative villous-architecture plate without implying a biopsy grade.
- MeSH defines Cerebral Palsy as a heterogeneous group of nonprogressive motor disorders, requiring neutral system orientation rather than a universal lesion, posture, or subtype.
- MeSH defines Cholestasis broadly as impaired bile flow through small intrahepatic or large extrahepatic ducts, so the prompt depicts both pathways and asserts no single obstruction cause.
- CDC states that no confirmatory test currently diagnoses ME/CFS and that proposed biomarkers lack sufficient sensitivity and specificity. The image therefore remains neutral multisystem orientation and avoids an exhaustion or energy metaphor.
- The Barrett and Breast Cancer prompts are independent revision-2 records. Their Batch 046 source/failure evidence remains immutable and non-public.
