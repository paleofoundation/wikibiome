## Queue and identity decisions

The supplied queue was audited before prompt creation against local canonical routes, the identity registry, current NCBI Taxonomy and LPSN records, and primary or type-anchored morphology sources. DOI, title, and PMID mappings were cross-checked where available. Authorities provide factual constraints only; no source image, micrograph, or design is copied. 1. `rothia` — **cleared.** Current correct genus *Rothia*, NCBITaxon:32207; type species *Rothia dentocariosa*. NCBI and LPSN retain the genus, and the Brown et al. primary identification paper, PMID 4886858 / PMCID PMC377629 / DOI `10.1128/am.17.1.150-156.1969`, exactly maps to “Laboratory identification of Rothia dentocariosa and its occurrence in human clinical materials.” The proposed plate uses a restrained type-species-anchored selection of irregular short rods and ovoid/coccoid bodies, representative and non-diagnostic rather than universal across the genus.
2. `ruminococcaceae` — **scientific/editorial hold; no prompt or raster.** LPSN records *Ruminococcaceae* Rainey 2010 as validly published but illegitimate and a synonym whose correct family name is *Oscillospiraceae*. NCBI merged former Taxonomy ID 541000 into current *Oscillospiraceae*, NCBITaxon:216572, on 2021-03-15 and lists *Ruminococcaceae* as a heterotypic synonym. The canonical `oscillospiraceae` route already owns reviewed media. Proposed disposition: retire or permanently alias `ruminococcaceae` to `oscillospiraceae`; do not duplicate the scientific entity or its media.
3. `ruminococcus` — **cleared.** Current correct genus *Ruminococcus*, NCBITaxon:1263; type species *Ruminococcus flavefaciens*. NCBI and LPSN retain the genus under current family *Oscillospiraceae*. La Reau et al. 2016, PMID 28348838 / PMCID PMC5359413 / DOI `10.1099/mgen.0.000099`, exactly maps to “Sequence-based analysis of the genus Ruminococcus resolves its phylogeny and reveals strong host association” and documents the genus's phylogenetic complexity. The proposed coccoid singles, pairs, and short-chain plate is explicitly type-species/current-circumscription anchored, representative, non-universal, and non-diagnostic; appendages are omitted without making a motility claim.
4. `ruminococcus-albus` — **scientific/editorial hold; no prompt or raster.** NCBI Taxonomy ID 1264 now displays current *Hominimerdicola alba*. LPSN records *Hominimerdicola alba* (Hungate 1957) Hisatomi et al. 2026 as the valid correct combination, with *Ruminococcus albus* as the basonym and type strain 7 / ATCC 27210 / DSM 20455 / JCM 14654. Proposed disposition: canonicalize the current entity as `hominimerdicola-alba`, NCBITaxon:1264, and retain `ruminococcus-albus` only as an asset-free audited permanent alias.
5. `ruminococcus-bromii` — **cleared.** Current correct species *Ruminococcus bromii*, NCBITaxon:40518; type strain ATCC 27255. NCBI and LPSN retain the species under *Oscillospiraceae*. DOI `10.1099/00207713-22-2-78` exactly maps to Moore et al. 1972, “Ruminococcus bromii sp. n. and Emendation of the Description of Ruminococcus Sijpestein,” supporting conservative coccoid cells in singles, pairs, and short arrangements. The species plate remains representative and non-diagnostic and does not claim illustration-level separability from the genus plate.
6. `ruminococcus-gnavus` — **scientific/editorial hold; no prompt or raster.** NCBI Taxonomy ID 33038 now displays current *Mediterraneibacter gnavus*. LPSN records *Mediterraneibacter gnavus* as the valid correct and recommended combination, with *Ruminococcus gnavus* as its basonym. DOI `10.1007/s10482-018-1104-y` maps to the reclassification paper establishing *Mediterraneibacter gnavus* comb. nov. Proposed disposition: canonicalize the current entity as `mediterraneibacter-gnavus`, NCBITaxon:33038, and retain `ruminococcus-gnavus` only as an asset-free audited permanent alias.
