## Authority and representation boundary

- NLM MeSH 2026 descriptors: Infertility, Female `D007247`; Fibromyalgia `D005356`; Stomach Neoplasms `D013274`; Adenocarcinoma `D000230`; Stomach Ulcer `D013276`; Gastroesophageal Reflux `D005764`.
- Female infertility has heterogeneous ovulatory, tubal, uterine, endocrine, age-related, and other contributors. The plate therefore presents complete neutral anatomy and ovarian-cycle orientation without a blockage, lesion, ovarian-reserve claim, or implied cause.
- Fibromyalgia is a heterogeneous chronic widespread pain syndrome without a single visible tissue lesion or universally established imaging biomarker. The plate keeps brain, network, spinal, and muscle structures neutral and undamaged.
- Gastric adenocarcinoma is the predominant histologic form of stomach cancer and begins in glandular cells of the mucosal lining. The route-specific plate confines a representative gland-forming focus to a separate mucosal teaching segment and makes no stage or invasion claim.
- Gastric cancer is the broader site-defined condition and includes rarer non-adenocarcinoma malignancies. Its plate uses one nonspecific mucosal growth and deliberately omits glandular architecture so it does not collapse the broad route into the adenocarcinoma route.
- Gastric ulcer is ulceration of gastric mucosa. The plate shows one small non-perforated, non-bleeding mucosal crater without implying Helicobacter pylori, NSAID use, or another cause.
- Gastroesophageal reflux is retrograde movement of gastric or duodenal contents into the distal esophagus. The plate depicts a short contained luminal flow at the junction without asserting mucosal damage, complication, or a universal sphincter defect.
