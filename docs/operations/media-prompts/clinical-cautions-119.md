## Route audits

### `stop-nickel-rich-foods-snas`

MeSH D009532 identifies Nickel but not the page's many foods, their variable concentrations, servings, analytical sources, allergy phenotypes, dietary thresholds, or treatment protocols. A collage would imply a fixed prohibited-food list; the reviewed Nickel material specimen would not depict dietary exposure or a diet. The page needs a dated authoritative food-composition source and one stable non-prescriptive subject before media can be considered.

### `stop-nsaids-ckd`

MeSH D000894 identifies non-steroidal anti-inflammatory agents and D051436 identifies chronic renal insufficiency, but the single linked CKD microbiome review does not define an agent-level prescribing decision. Indication, formulation, route, dose, duration, kidney stage and function, volume status, interactions, monitoring, outcomes, alternatives, and exceptions remain unresolved. Pill, kidney, pain, warning, or prohibition imagery would falsely universalize the claim.

### `stop-probiotics-without-stress-management-ovarian-cancer`

MeSH D019936 identifies probiotics and D010051 identifies ovarian neoplasms. The only linked source is an animal-model thesis, and the page acknowledges absent human evidence. It does not identify the full probiotic product, a human stress assessment or intervention, cancer subtype or stage, anticancer treatment, outcomes, safety, or exceptions. Patient, tumor, ovary, cortisol, wellness, or microbe imagery would overstate translation and risk stigma.

### `stop-broad-spectrum-antibiotics-pre-nec`

MeSH D000900 identifies anti-bacterial agents and D020345 identifies necrotizing enterocolitis, a condition seen primarily in low-birth-weight infants. The route has zero linked sources and an explicit hypothesis disclaimer. Gestational age, birth weight, infection assessment, agent, route, dose, duration, unit protocol, feeding, comparator, NEC definition, sepsis, mortality, safety, and exceptions are unresolved. No neonatal clinical scene or treatment image is ethically or scientifically publishable from this record.

### `stop-scfa-replacement-without-dysbiosis-restoration-ms`

MeSH D005232 groups volatile or short-chain fatty acids, and D009103 identifies multiple sclerosis. The page nevertheless combines several compounds, microbial groups, ecological restoration, ketogenic diet, B-cell depletion, bile-acid support, and neurologic outcomes. Compound, formulation, dose, taxa or strains, restoration product, MS phenotype and activity, concurrent therapy, comparator, methods, outcomes, safety, and exceptions remain unresolved. An MS model, metabolite diagram, or microorganism portrait would show only one component and misrepresent the protocol.

### `stop-standard-probiotics-endometriosis`

MeSH D019936 identifies probiotics and D004715 identifies endometriosis, but “standard” or “unselected” probiotic is not a stable product. The route combines estrobolome, Candida, metal ecology, low-nickel diet, lactoferrin, and N-acetylcysteine. Strains, formulation, dose, timing, endometriosis phenotype and stage, fertility and hormonal context, comparator, methods, outcomes, safety, and exceptions remain unresolved. Existing anatomy, microbe, element, supplement, or warning media cannot stand in for this combined protocol.
