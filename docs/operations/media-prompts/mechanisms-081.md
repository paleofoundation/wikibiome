## Route audits

### `metal-speciation`

IUPAC defines speciation as the distribution of an element among defined chemical species and speciation analysis as the analytical identification or quantification of individual species. The page instead treats free ion, ligand complex, protein binding, mineral phase, oxidation state, solubility, absorption, and biological effect as a single deterministic ladder. It also repeats outdated or unsafe simplifications, including trivalent chromium as an essential nutrient and unqualified comparisons of arsenic oxidation states. A publishable representation requires one named element, matrix, temperature, pH, redox condition, ligands, time scale, sampling preservation, separation/detection method, and operationally defined species; otherwise an image would falsely imply stable, directly observed pools.

Authorities: https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/ST06861 ; https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/ST06848 ; https://publications.iupac.org/pac/72/8/1453/index.html ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39532431/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/metal-speciation

### `metallochaperone`

Current reviews describe metal allocation by diverse metallochaperones to client proteins or compartments and note that some energy-requiring systems do not necessarily insert metal by direct contact. The route says metallochaperones universally escort ions from entry to a target, physically hand off metals, bypass the labile pool, and provide the kinetic solution to thermodynamics. It then merges bacterial and eukaryotic copper systems, nickel enzyme-maturation complexes, iron-delivery proteins, extracellular metallophores, host intoxication, and proposed therapeutic disruption. Rebuild by organism, named protein/accession or structure, metal species, client, compartment, stoichiometry, nucleotide dependence, demonstrated interaction, and assay before choosing any molecular plate.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28487396/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39658019/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/metallochaperone

### `metalloestrogen`

This singular route is not a distinct entity: it explicitly redirects readers to the plural route for the same mechanism while retaining its own canonical article. It states that cadmium, nickel, and copper measured in disease signatures may directly drive estrogenic signaling; assigns evidence grades and receptor activities across several metals; and converts small or indirect observations in breast cancer, endometriosis, and polycystic ovary syndrome into mechanistic explanations. The cited critical review found persuasive in-vitro and in-vivo cadmium evidence but conflicting population evidence, and the cadmium/nickel review says chronic low-level carcinogenesis through estrogen receptors remains unclear. Reconcile the duplicate canonical identity and separate ion/species, receptor, assay, concentration, model, tissue, and human outcome before media generation.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22161274/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22970719/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/metalloestrogen ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/metalloestrogens

### `metalloestrogens`

The plural page treats many metals as one endocrine-disrupting class and moves between receptor binding, reporter assays, transformed cell lines, animal findings, tissue concentrations, allergy observations, reproductive conditions, and cancer risk. It presents exact affinities and broad disease implications without consistently distinguishing metal salt or species, exposure concentration, assay system, receptor subtype, model, tissue, route, dose, or epidemiological certainty. Even for cadmium—the best studied candidate—human population evidence remains conflicting; for nickel and other ions it is weaker or assay-dependent. Merge or clearly distinguish the duplicate route, grade evidence per chemical species and endpoint, and separate hypothesis from established human effect before creating a receptor or disease image.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10770491/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12873394/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22161274/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22970719/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/metalloestrogens

### `metallomics`

Metallomics spans total-element measurement, isotope analysis, spatial mapping, chemical speciation, and metal-containing molecules; each requires different preparation, instrumentation, calibration, interference control, reference materials, and interpretation. The page correctly notes that its cited disease panels are small discovery studies, but elsewhere labels recurring cancer patterns universal, describes three tiers of diagnostic power, and repeats very high AUC values as disease detection or differentiation without independent prospective validation. It also blends clinical biofluids, post-mortem tissue, infection metallophores, microbial ecology, genetics, and speculative integrated signatures. Rebuild around specimen, analyte definition, preanalytics, platform, calibration/QC, batch correction, multiplicity, model validation, external replication, and intended use before an instrument or diagnostic image is considered.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34048228/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39532431/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7741231/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/metallomics

### `metalloregulator`

Metalloregulators comprise multiple protein families whose metal selectivity, allosteric response, regulon, and physiology are organism-specific. The page converts examples into universal rules: ligand donor type alone determines cognate metal; weak and strong binders split into kinetic versus thermodynamic sensing; all sensors are co-evolved and one disruption collapses the network; importers lack specificity; and blocking selected regulators predictably disables pathogens. Reviews instead emphasize family diversity, selective-perception mechanisms, non-cognate metals, stepwise responses, and organism-specific host interactions. Rebuild by organism/strain, regulator/accession or structure, family, metal species, binding sites/stoichiometry, operator/regulon, assay conditions, mutant phenotype, and in-vivo evidence; keep therapeutic targeting and co-selection explicitly investigational.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16158234/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17216355/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25160631/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28344348/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39658019/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/metalloregulator
