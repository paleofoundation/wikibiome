## Authority and representation boundary

- Gingipains are controlled by NLM MeSH `D000080867`, MEROPS family `C25`, EC `3.4.22.37` and `3.4.22.47`, and representative RgpB/Kgp experimental structures `PDB:1CVR` and `PDB:4RBM`. They are cysteine endopeptidases, not zinc-dependent metalloproteases. The plate therefore contains no metal and represents only two resolved fragments; RgpA and full-length architectures remain outside scope.
- Glyoxalase I is NLM MeSH `D007791`, Gene Ontology `GO:0004462`, EC `4.4.1.5`, and Rhea `19069`. The plate is explicitly limited to the nickel-bound *Escherichia coli* K-12 GloA exemplar `UniProt:P0AC81` / `PDB:1F9Z`. Metal activation varies across enzymes; the local host-zinc/pathogen-nickel rule and universal-pathogen claims do not control the media.
- `gut-barrier-dysfunction` has no exact MeSH descriptor and remains a WikiBiome canonical concept. NLM MeSH `D000095644` Intestinal Barrier Function, NLM MeSH `D007413` Intestinal Mucosa, and Gene Ontology `GO:0030277` are related contexts, not proof of a dysfunction state. Two modest clefts do not establish translocation, causality, diagnosis, or severity.
- `gut-microbiome` is an exact entry term under NLM MeSH `D000069196`, Gastrointestinal Microbiome. Human metagenomic studies establish complexity and variation, not one universal composition; the separate silhouettes and counts are non-taxonomic layout constraints only.
- `gut-brain-axis` is an exact entry term under NLM MeSH `D000087502`, Brain-Gut Axis. The controlled scope is an interactive network. The plate separates brain, gut, and one neural-route context to avoid claiming an anatomically traced vagus/enteric circuit, direction, exhaustiveness, microbial causality, or a clinical effect.
