## Route audits

### `siderophore-competition`

- Identity: NLM MeSH `D017262` identifies siderophores as a class of low-molecular-weight microbial compounds involved in ferric-iron transport and sequestration. There is no separate controlled object or canonical physical configuration for “siderophore competition.”
- Scope conflict: the article combines multiple bacterial and fungal producers, chemically distinct siderophores, ferric chelation, uptake receptors, siderophore piracy, host lipocalin-2, commensal iron strategies, infection contexts, antibiotic conjugates, and chelation interventions.
- Representation risk: a two-organism “iron war” scene would invent a universal spatial and causal configuration; one siderophore would arbitrarily stand for a broader ecological process.
- Required resolution: choose exact competing organisms and strains, siderophore identity and stereochemistry, iron species and occupancy, medium and spatial arrangement, concentrations, time point, receptor context, assay, and measured outcome.
- Authorities: https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D017262 ; https://www.wikibiome.com/article/siderophore-competition

### `siderophores`

- Identity: MeSH `D017262` defines a chemical-function class, not one molecular graph. Members include catecholate, hydroxamate, carboxylate, mixed-type, peptide, and other scaffolds with distinct stereochemistry, metal selectivity, and complexes.
- Scope conflict: the page spans enterobactin, salmochelin, yersiniabactin, aerobactin, pyoverdine, pyochelin, staphyloferrins, mycobactins, fungal siderophores, host sequestration, receptors, diagnostics, and antimicrobial applications.
- Representation risk: a generic chelator or decorative iron-binding molecule would not be a verifiable chemical identity; a montage would imply a complete or canonical taxonomy.
- Required resolution: select one exact compound with controlled chemical identity, stereochemistry and protonation state, producer strain, apo or specified ferric complex, stoichiometry, structure source, compartment, receptor context, assay, and claim boundary.
- Authorities: https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D017262 ; https://www.wikibiome.com/article/siderophores

- Identity: MeSH `D013482` identifies superoxide dismutase, EC `1.15.1.1`, as an enzyme class. The descriptor includes or relates to copper-, zinc-, manganese-, iron-, cobalt-, and other forms rather than one universal protein structure.

### `transferrin`

- Identity: MeSH `D014168` identifies transferrin; reviewed UniProt `P02787` identifies human serotransferrin, a 698-residue precursor whose mature glycoprotein binds two ferric ions with an associated anion.
- State conflict: the page combines apo-, monoferric-, and diferric transferrin, receptor-mediated uptake and endosomal release, glycosylation, serum measurements, other-metal binding, host defense, disease associations, and microbial iron acquisition. - Required resolution: select exact species and sequence, isoform and mature chain, glycoform, apo/monoferric/diferric state, ferric-site occupancy and synergistic anion, oligomer and structure accession, retained partners, compartment or specimen, assay, and claim boundary.
- Authorities: https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D014168 ; https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P02787/entry ; https://www.wikibiome.com/article/transferrin
