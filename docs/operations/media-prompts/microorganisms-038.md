## Identity and morphology evidence

- `subdoligranulum`: current genus `NCBITaxon:292632`; type species *Subdoligranulum variabile*, type strain BI 114 / CCUG 47106 / DSM 15176. NCBI Taxonomy and LPSN confirm the name and type. Holmstrøm et al. describe the type isolate as strictly anaerobic, non-spore-forming, Gram-negative-staining, and variably coccus-shaped. The plate therefore shows separated, slightly variable coccoid cells and makes no Gram-status color claim.
- `sutterella`: current genus `NCBITaxon:40544`; type species *Sutterella wadsworthensis*, type strain WAL 9799 / ATCC 51579. LPSN and Wexler et al. confirm the genus and type. Published scanning-electron-microscopy work documents pleomorphism, predominantly long rods and coccobacilli. The plate conservatively represents those two forms without lattice, host, or disease context.
- `torulaspora`: current genus `NCBITaxon:4948`; type species *Torulaspora delbrueckii*, represented by neotype culture CBS 1146 / NRRL Y-866. NCBI classifies the taxon among budding yeasts. Modern taxonomic descriptions of the type species document multilateral budding and asci containing one or two smooth spherical ascospores. The plate uses only vegetative ovoid cells and buds, avoiding a genus-wide sporulation claim.
- `treponema`: current genus `NCBITaxon:157`; type species *Treponema pallidum*. LPSN confirms the current genus and type species. CDC describes the type species as a thin, flexuous, flat-wave spirochete; periplasmic endoflagella drive corkscrew-like motility but are not external appendages. The plate therefore shows smooth slender wave-form bodies without exposed flagella or disease imagery.
- `trichoderma`: current genus `NCBITaxon:5543`; type species *Trichoderma viride*. NCBI confirms the current genus. CDC PHIL and the ATCC type-species record describe septate hyphae and branched conidiophores bearing flask-shaped phialides topped by small clusters of globose conidia. The plate isolates one restrained representative conidiophore system rather than showing a colony or habitat.
- `trichomonas-vaginalis`: current species `NCBITaxon:5722`; no cyst stage. CDC DPDx describes pyriform trophozoites 7–30 micrometres long with four anterior flagella, a fifth recurrent flagellum running along the undulating membrane, and one anterior nucleus. The plate depicts one non-diagnostic trophozoite with those externally visible structural relationships and no host tissue or clinical scene.
