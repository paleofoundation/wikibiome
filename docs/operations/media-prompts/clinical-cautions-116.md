## Route audits

### `stop-iron-supplementation-cardiovascular-disease`

The page does not select a cardiovascular diagnosis, ejection-fraction phenotype, iron-deficiency or anemia definition, oral or intravenous route, compound, elemental dose, duration, or clinical outcome. Its five sources concern microbiome, TMAO, iron homeostasis, or metabolites rather than a clinical withholding protocol. The 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA guideline considers intravenous iron reasonable for selected patients with reduced-ejection-fraction heart failure and iron deficiency. Any heart, plaque, blood, tablet, infusion, or prohibition image would suppress that indication-specific boundary.
