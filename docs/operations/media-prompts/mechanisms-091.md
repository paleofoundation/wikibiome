## Route audits

### `serotonin-estrogen-axis`

The route is a proposed synthesis rather than one controlled biological entity. It combines central and peripheral serotonin pools, multiple estrogen species and receptors, tryptophan pathways, enterochromaffin signaling, microbial beta-glucuronidases, menstrual stages, and four clinical conditions into a universal bidirectional axis. Gut-derived serotonin is largely peripheral and must not be visually implied to enter the brain; disease-specific hormonal and serotonergic findings need separate evidence. Split by compartment, hormone/metabolite, receptor/enzyme, cell, cycle or life stage, specimen, assay, disease phenotype, intervention, and evidence tier.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34502396/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/serotonin-estrogen-axis

### `short-chain-fatty-acids`

The class includes distinct acids, conjugate states, production and consumption pathways, compartments, receptors, concentrations, and context-dependent effects. The page asserts near-universal disease depletion and a universal metal–SCFA vicious cycle, merges fecal abundance with production and systemic exposure, assigns broad taxa as fixed producers, and generalizes barrier, immune, brain, cardiovascular, and cancer effects. Split by exact analyte and matrix with concentration/units, sampling/processing, analytical method, substrate, organism/pathway, host receptor or target, model/population, endpoint, and certainty.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28300535/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36362272/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/short-chain-fatty-acids

### `shotgun-metagenomics`

Shotgun metagenomics is a multi-step family of workflows, not a single direct readout of who is present and what they do. The page gives universal costs/depths, promises species/strain and multi-kingdom resolution, calls gene content direct functional measurement, assigns one method exclusive reliability for metal genes, and generalizes sample host-DNA percentages. Results depend on specimen, extraction, library, platform, depth, controls, host depletion, database/version, profiler/assembler, thresholds, and validation; DNA indicates functional potential rather than expression or activity. Rebuild as a method record with explicit workflow and reporting standard.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32768390/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37731336/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42014453/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/shotgun-metagenomics

### `srna`

Small regulatory RNAs are a heterogeneous set spanning cis- and trans-encoded RNAs, different sizes, chaperone dependencies, binding modes, organisms, targets, and phenotypes. The page recasts the entire field as rapid post-transcriptional metal-starvation coordination, then merges RsaC, PrrF, Ccn, NikS, and RusT systems and speculative therapeutic conclusions. Split by exact organism, sRNA locus/sequence/accession, transcriptional regulator, chaperone, direct RNA target and validated interaction, metal condition/concentration, assay, phenotype, and evidence tier.

Authorities: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24003243/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/srna

### `statins`

Statins are chemically and pharmacokinetically distinct drugs and formulations. The page generalizes in-vitro antimicrobial findings, individual observational/intervention studies, genetic proxies, taxa changes, bile-acid hypotheses, metal absorption, neurodegeneration, cancer, and adverse effects into a class-wide microbiome mechanism. Mendelian-randomization genetic instruments are not equivalent to administered statin exposure. Split by exact drug, dose, formulation, route, indication, population/model, duration, specimen, microbiome method, pharmacokinetic endpoint, comparator, clinical outcome, and evidence tier.

Authorities: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/68019161 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39734574/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/statins

### `succinate`

Succinate dianion is identifiable as PubChem CID 160419, distinct from succinic acid CID 1110, but the page merges host mitochondrial, immune-cell, extracellular, luminal microbial, and brain pools. It presents context-dependent SUCNR1 and HIF signaling as uniformly pro-inflammatory, calls Dialister a primary producer, and asserts that Dialister depletion causes hippocampal mitochondrial and mood deficits without route-matched evidence. Split by chemical species, compartment, source organism/cell and pathway, concentration/units, sample and assay, receptor/target, model/population, outcome, and evidence tier.

Authorities: https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Succinate%20dianion ; https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/1110 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30583500/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41080558/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/succinate
