- `gut-metal-microbiome` has no exact current MeSH or OLS class and remains a WikiBiome composite. Human studies are small and heterogeneous; one study found no significant stool-community differences across prostatic disease groups, and a 2026 study of 28 participants found no alpha- or beta-diversity separation. A 24-participant post-antibiotic probiotic trial cannot establish a general prostate-axis treatment effect. The plate therefore shows normal component anatomy only.
- `gut-thyroid-axis` has no exact current MeSH or OLS class and remains a WikiBiome composite. NLM MeSH `D013961` and `UBERON:0002046` identify the thyroid component, not an axis; Hashimoto and Graves descriptors are disease contexts only. Human cohorts and animal transfer/manipulation studies support a research domain, not universal taxa or causal direction. Primary evidence supports bacterial deconjugation of biliary thyroid-hormone conjugates in rats, not a secure general bacterial T4-to-T3 pathway; the plate therefore shows no hormone, conversion, autoimmune state, or direction.
