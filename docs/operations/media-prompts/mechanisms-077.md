## Route audits

### `infant-exposure`

This is not one controlled exposure, developmental stage, specimen, or endpoint. The page combines prenatal transfer, breast milk, formula, complementary foods, environmental contact, and parenteral nutrition; then assigns common barrier, microbiome, neurological, NEC, autism, and lifelong effects across metals and routes. It also presents “prenatal metal chelation” as an open intervention question. FDA's current lead guidance is product-category specific, nonbinding, and explicitly excludes infant formula and beverages; CDC guidance limits chelation in pregnancy or early infancy to confirmed very high blood lead levels with expert consultation. Separate age, route, analyte/species, dose, product, geography, endpoint, and evidence level; remove generalized chelation framing and reconcile the overlapping infant-food and parenteral-nutrition routes before any representative image is considered.

### `insulin-resistance`

MeSH D007333 identifies insulin resistance, but the article is largely a PCOS-metal-microbiome synthesis. It presents chromium as an established essential component of a glucose-tolerance system, assigns zinc depletion and toxic metals broad causal roles, and converts heterogeneous association and experimental findings into dietary, probiotic, ketogenic, and supplementation implications. A pancreas, receptor pathway, adipocyte, ovary, or whole-body plate would each imply a narrower mechanism than the article supports. Rebuild around operational definitions and validated measurement, separate tissue-specific physiology from syndromic contexts, distinguish association from causation and human from model evidence, and remove intervention advice that is not guideline-grounded before selecting a representation.

Authorities: https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D007333 ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/insulin-resistance

### `interleukin-6`

The exact human protein is MeSH D015850 and reviewed UniProt P05231. IL-6 has context-dependent roles in immunity, regeneration, metabolism, infection, injury, exercise, and disease. Classic, trans-, and cluster signaling depend on receptor form, cell type, concentration, timing, and regulatory context; even reviews describing a classic-protective/trans-inflammatory tendency warn that the biology is more complex. The page instead assigns a broadly protective classic arm and pathological trans arm, calls heavy metals potent IL-6 inducers whose effects are molecularly indistinguishable, attributes fixed fractions of disease causality, and moves into zinc, iron, and therapeutic implications. Rebuild around the exact protein, receptor complexes, signaling modes, cell and tissue contexts, and evidence-bounded disease examples before choosing a molecular structure or pathway plate.

Authorities: https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D015850 ; https://www.uniprot.org/uniprot/P05231.html ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24986424/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37069261/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/interleukin-6

### `intestinal-permeability`

MeSH D000095644 places intestinal permeability as a narrower concept under intestinal barrier function, encompassing transport through the intestinal mucosa rather than one lesion or disease. The article calls it a gateway to virtually every disease, presents zonulin as the only physiological regulator and a practical serum biomarker, infers causality from temporal association, and recommends probiotics, zinc, and metal removal. Commercial zonulin ELISAs may not detect pre-haptoglobin-2, serum zonulin did not correlate with a sugar-permeability test in one human study, and contemporary reviews emphasize method-specific limitations. Define paracellular and transcellular processes, site and probe, separate barrier injury from permeability measurement, remove universal causal chains, and distinguish research tools from validated clinical tests and treatments before any intestinal plate is considered.

Authorities: https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D000095644 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29459849/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32563954/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34513903/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/intestinal-permeability

### `iron-sulfur-clusters`

MeSH D007506 describes iron-sulfur proteins, while the route ranges across [2Fe-2S], [4Fe-4S], and other cofactors, assembly systems, proteins, compartments, bacteria, host biology, and disease. Fe-S clusters are diverse and can be damaged by oxidants and toxic levels of some metals, but this does not support the page's claim that they are the primary intracellular target of copper and nickel rather than DNA or lipids, its universal Ni/Cd/Ag/Ga mechanism, or its organism-by-organism dependency table. Some nickel-containing metalloenzymes also function alongside Fe-S relays rather than merely destroying them. Specify cluster type, protein, organism, compartment, assembly pathway, exposure, concentration, and evidence; remove universal damage and microbiome-dependency claims before choosing a molecular or cellular representation.

Authorities: https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?ui=D007506 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26488283/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38744662/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29019354/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/iron-sulfur-clusters

### `irving-williams-series`

The Irving-Williams series is a useful trend in divalent first-row transition-metal complex stability, not an assertion that copper displaces every metal from every site or that one affinity order alone predicts biology. Cellular metalation depends on oxidation state, ligand and geometry, competition, buffered availability, transport, delivery, kinetics, compartment, and protein maturation; cells often maintain metal availabilities approximately inverse to the affinity trend and use additional selectivity mechanisms. The page extends the series to lead and cadmium, assigns exact universal free-metal hierarchies, treats nickel displacement of iron/cobalt and Fe-S clusters as automatic, and uses the series to explain broad disease categories. Bound the chemical domain, distinguish thermodynamic trends from cellular availability and kinetics, handle non-series ions separately, and verify each biological application before a coordination or metalation diagram is considered.

Authorities: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7239837/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36124565/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35236987/ ; local:data/content.generated.json#/pages/irving-williams-series
