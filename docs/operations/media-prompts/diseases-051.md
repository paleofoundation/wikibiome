## Authority and representation boundary

- NLM MeSH 2026 descriptors: Diabetes, Gestational `D016640`; Graves Disease `D006111`; Hashimoto Disease `D050031`; Hepatitis `D006505`; Hidradenitis Suppurativa `D017497`; HIV Infections `D015658`.
- Gestational diabetes is diabetes first developing during pregnancy and is distinct from pre-existing diabetes in pregnancy. The plate uses neutral pregnancy and glucose-regulation anatomy without a fetus, complication, or asserted mechanism.
- Graves disease is autoimmune hyperthyroidism mediated by TSH-receptor antibodies. Diffuse thyroid enlargement can occur, while ophthalmopathy affects only a subset, so the plate excludes eyes and presents representative thyroid and receptor orientation only.
- Hashimoto disease is autoimmune thyroiditis that can damage the gland and cause hypothyroidism. Gland size and clinical expression vary over time, so the plate avoids universal enlargement, atrophy, or gross destruction.
- Hepatitis is liver inflammation with viral and nonviral causes. The plate is deliberately etiology-neutral and cannot distinguish cause, chronicity, fibrosis, or severity.
- Hidradenitis suppurativa is a chronic, noncontagious inflammatory condition beginning in the hair follicle; tunnels and scarring can develop. One restrained follicle-centered lesion block is representative rather than a universal stage.
- HIV Infections covers the spectrum from asymptomatic infection through AIDS. The plate separates a lymph node, CD4-cell model, and HIV teaching particle without depicting entry, replication, transmission, stage, or a patient.
