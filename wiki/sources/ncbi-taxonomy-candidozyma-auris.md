# NCBI Taxonomy — Candidozyma auris

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 498019 lists *Candidozyma auris* (Satoh & Makimura) Q.M. Wang, Yurkov, Boekhout & F.Y. Bai 2024 as the current name and *Candida auris* Satoh & Makimura 2009 as its basionym. The record identifies JCM 15448, CBS 10913, and DSM 21092 as culture deposits derived from the holotype.

## Editorial Use

This database record supplies the stable NCBITaxon identifier and current-name relationship. NCBI advises consulting nomenclatural literature for authoritative classification, so WikiBiome also cites Index Fungorum and the primary 2024 combination.
