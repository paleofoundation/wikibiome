# Copeland 2009 — Atopobium parvulum type-strain genome

## Study Scope

This paper reports the complete genome of type strain IPP 1246 under the then-current name *Atopobium parvulum*. It describes the organism as an obligately anaerobic member of the former *Atopobium/Olsenella* branch and notes frequent isolation from the human oral cavity and an association with oral malodor.

## Current Name

The organism is now classified as [[lancefieldella-parvula]]. The historical name is retained here because it is the name used in the paper.
