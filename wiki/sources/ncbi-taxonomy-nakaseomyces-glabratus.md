# NCBI Taxonomy — Nakaseomyces glabratus

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 5478 lists *Nakaseomyces glabratus* (H.W. Anderson) Sugita & Takashima 2022 as the current name. It records *Candida glabrata* and *Torulopsis glabrata* as homotypic synonyms and *Cryptococcus glabratus* H.W. Anderson 1917 as the basionym. The type-material list includes ATCC 2001, CBS 138, JCM 3761, and equivalent deposits.

This database record supplies the stable NCBITaxon identifier and synonym relationship. WikiBiome also cites Index Fungorum and the primary 2022 combination for nomenclatural authority.
