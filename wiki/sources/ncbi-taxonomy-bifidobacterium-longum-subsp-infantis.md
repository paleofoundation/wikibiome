# NCBI Taxonomy — Bifidobacterium longum subsp. infantis

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 1682 lists *Bifidobacterium longum* subsp. *infantis* (Reuter 1963) Mattarelli et al. 2008 as the current name and *Bifidobacterium infantis* Reuter 1963 as its basionym. The record gives the rank as subspecies and includes ATCC 15697, DSM 20088, and JCM 1222 among the equivalent type-strain deposits.

## Editorial Use

This database record supplies the stable NCBITaxon identifier and current-name relationship. NCBI itself advises consulting nomenclatural literature for authoritative classification, so WikiBiome also cites LPSN and the primary 2008 reclassification.
