# LPSN — Lancefieldella parvula

## Record Scope

LPSN lists *Lancefieldella parvula* (Weinberg et al. 1937) Nouioui et al. 2018 as a validly published correct name and the type species of *Lancefieldella*. It records *Atopobium parvulum*, *Streptococcus parvulus*, and *Peptostreptococcus parvulus* as homotypic synonyms.

## Editorial Use

This record supports nomenclatural reconciliation and the historical-name note.
