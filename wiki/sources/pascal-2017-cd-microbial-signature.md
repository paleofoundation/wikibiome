## Study summary

This multicohort observational study profiled bacterial and archaeal 16S rRNA sequences and validated a Crohn's disease microbial signature against several independent cohorts. WikiBiome uses it as clinical evidence for reproducible organism-level depletion and enrichment patterns in Crohn's disease.
