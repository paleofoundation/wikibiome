# NCBI Taxonomy — Cladosporium

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 5498 represents the fungal genus *Cladosporium*. It supplies the stable NCBITaxon identifier used by WikiBiome and places current *Cladosporium* species beneath that genus.

## Editorial Use

NCBI supplies the controlled identifier, not the governing nomenclatural decision. WikiBiome therefore cites Species Fungorum and the ICTF working group's primary recommendation for the accepted-name relationship with *Davidiella*.
