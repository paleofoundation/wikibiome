# Rosero et al. 2016 — *Agathobacter rectalis* comb. nov.

## Taxonomic Finding

Rosero and colleagues proposed the new genus *Agathobacter* and transferred *Eubacterium rectale* to it as *Agathobacter rectalis* comb. nov. Their analysis used phenotypic and chemotaxonomic characterization together with 16S rRNA and the housekeeping genes *dnaK*, *groEL*, and *atpA*.

## Editorial Boundary

This is the primary publication for the current combination selected by LPSN and NCBI. A later letter and whole-genome analysis challenged the phylogenetic adequacy of the transfer, so WikiBiome does not describe the placement as undisputed.
