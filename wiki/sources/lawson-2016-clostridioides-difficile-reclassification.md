# Lawson et al. 2016 — Reclassification of Clostridium difficile

## Publication Scope

Lawson and colleagues compared 16S rRNA sequence, phenotype, chemotaxonomy, and phylogenetic placement. They found that *Clostridium difficile* and *C. mangenotii* formed a lineage in Peptostreptococcaceae far removed from *Clostridium butyricum* and other members of *Clostridium* sensu stricto.

## Nomenclatural Result

The authors proposed *Clostridioides* as a new genus, transferred *Clostridium difficile* to the new combination *Clostridioides difficile*, and designated it as the type species. The paper supports the name-change relationship; it does not alter or retrospectively rewrite the organism label printed in older studies.
