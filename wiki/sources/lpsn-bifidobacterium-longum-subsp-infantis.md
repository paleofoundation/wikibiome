# LPSN — Bifidobacterium longum subsp. infantis

## Record Scope

LPSN lists *Bifidobacterium longum* subsp. *infantis* (Reuter 1963) Mattarelli et al. 2008 as a validly published correct name. It records *Bifidobacterium infantis* Reuter 1963 as the basionym and homotypic synonym at a distinct rank, and identifies ATCC 15697, DSM 20088, JCM 1222, and equivalent deposits as the type strain.

## Editorial Use

This record supports the canonical title, rank, nomenclatural history, and type-strain equivalence. It does not establish the identity of commercial or study formulations reported only as “*B. infantis*.”
