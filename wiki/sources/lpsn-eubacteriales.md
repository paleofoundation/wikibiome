# LPSN — Eubacteriales

## Record Scope

LPSN lists *Eubacteriales* Buchanan 1917 (Approved Lists 1980) as a validly published order under the ICNP and as the correct name. It identifies *Eubacterium* Prévot 1938 (Approved Lists 1980) as the type genus.

## Synonym Boundary

LPSN records *Clostridiales* Prévot 1953 (Approved Lists 1980) as a validly published heterotypic synonym and directs the Clostridiales entry to *Eubacteriales* as the correct name. This relationship supports one canonical order record rather than two indexable taxa.
