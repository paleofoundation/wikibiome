# Sheridan et al. 2016 — Objection to the *Agathobacter rectalis* Transfer

## Scope

Sheridan and colleagues objected to the proposed transfer of *Eubacterium rectale* to *Agathobacter rectalis*. They argued that the evidence required closer assessment because the narrowed human-gut species definition and its established literature were not adequately represented by the proposed placement.

## Editorial Use

WikiBiome follows the current name selected by NCBI and LPSN while citing this letter as an explicit limitation. The objection does not create a second canonical species page; it explains why the current-name decision must not be described as universal scientific agreement.
