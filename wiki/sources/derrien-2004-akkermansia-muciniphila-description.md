# Derrien 2004 — Akkermansia muciniphila

## Study Scope

Derrien and colleagues isolated strain Muc from human feces in anaerobic medium containing gastric mucin as the sole carbon and nitrogen source. Culture and 16S rRNA analyses supported a new genus and species, *Akkermansia muciniphila*; Muc was deposited as ATCC BAA-835 and CIP 107961.

## Editorial Use

This is the primary description supporting the recognized species name and its mucin-degrading isolation phenotype.
