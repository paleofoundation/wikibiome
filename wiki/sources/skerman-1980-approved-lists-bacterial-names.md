# Skerman, McGowan, and Sneath 1980 — Approved Lists

## Publication Scope

The Approved Lists of Bacterial Names included *Eubacteriales* Buchanan 1917 and *Clostridiales* Prévot 1953 among the names retained under the Bacteriological Code from 1 January 1980.

## Editorial Use

The Approved Lists establish the valid publication status cited by LPSN. Current correct-name selection and the heterotypic-synonym relationship are taken from LPSN rather than inferred from the list alone.
