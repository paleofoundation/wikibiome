# Colonetti 2023 — Gut and Vaginal Microbiota in Endometriosis

## Review Scope

Colonetti and colleagues systematically reviewed 16 observational studies of intestinal and reproductive-tract microbiota in 1,151 women with and without endometriosis. Their table summarizes the Ata 2019 stool result using the genus spelling *Barnesiella*.

## Editorial Use

This review documents later normalization of the label printed as “Barnesella” by Ata and colleagues. It does not report reanalysis of the original sequence reads, so the normalized spelling is not treated as definitive resolution of the underlying taxonomic assignment.
