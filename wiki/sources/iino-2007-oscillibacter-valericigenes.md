# Iino et al. 2007 — *Oscillibacter valericigenes*

## Scope

Iino and colleagues described strain Sjm18-20T as the type strain of the new genus and species *Oscillibacter valericigenes*. The organism was isolated from the alimentary canal of a Japanese corbicula clam, not a freshwater fish.

## Morphology and Metabolism

The primary description reports Gram-negative, non-sporulating, straight to slightly curved rods measuring 2.5–6.0 micrometres in ordinary culture. Cells displayed oscillatory motility by means of peritrichous flagella and could elongate substantially after prolonged cultivation. Valeric acid was the major end product from glucose under the reported anaerobic growth conditions.

## Editorial Use

This paper supplies the type-species provenance and conservative morphology basis for [[oscillibacter]]. Genus-level imagery must remain representative and non-diagnostic: it may use the reported rod shape, but should not imply that every member shares the type strain's motility, dimensions, or metabolic phenotype.
