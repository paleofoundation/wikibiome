# LPSN — Fannyhessea vaginae

## Record Scope

LPSN lists *Fannyhessea vaginae* (Rodriguez Jovita et al. 1999) Nouioui et al. 2018 as a validly published correct name and the type species of *Fannyhessea*. It records *Atopobium vaginae* as a validly published homotypic synonym and basionym.

## Editorial Use

This record supports nomenclatural reconciliation and the historical-name note.
