# Index Fungorum — Nakaseomyces glabratus

## Record Scope

Index Fungorum connects *Cryptococcus glabratus* H.W. Anderson 1917, registration identifier 416322, to the current name *Nakaseomyces glabratus* (H.W. Anderson) Sugita & Takashima 2022. The record gives the original publication as Journal of Infectious Diseases 21:379.

## Editorial Use

This nomenclatural record supports the basionym, current combination, authorities, and publication trail. It does not support a separate canonical species under the former *Candida glabrata* combination.
