# Buchanan 1917 — The families of the Eubacteriales

## Publication Scope

Buchanan's 1917 paper established the order name *Eubacteriales* and described its family-level organization in the contemporary bacterial classification.

## Editorial Use

This is the effective original publication for the order name. It establishes nomenclatural priority over the later name *Clostridiales* but does not establish that Buchanan's 1917 circumscription is identical to every modern database assignment.
