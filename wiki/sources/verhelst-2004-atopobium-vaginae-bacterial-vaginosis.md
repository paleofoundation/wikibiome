# Verhelst 2004 — Atopobium vaginae and bacterial vaginosis

## Study Scope

Verhelst and colleagues compared cultured isolates and cloned 16S rRNA sequences from normal and disturbed vaginal microbiota. Under the historical species name *Atopobium vaginae*, they reported abundance in disturbed communities and a strong association with bacterial vaginosis, often together with *Gardnerella vaginalis*.

## Evidence Boundary

The study supports an association with bacterial vaginosis. It does not by itself establish disease causation, metal dependence, gut-to-vagina translocation, or treatment response.

## Current Name

The species is now classified as [[fannyhessea-vaginae]].
