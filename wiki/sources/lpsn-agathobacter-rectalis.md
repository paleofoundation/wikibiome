# LPSN — *Agathobacter rectalis*

## Record Scope

LPSN lists *Agathobacter rectalis* (Hauduroy et al. 1937) Rosero et al. 2016 as the correct, validly published combination and *Eubacterium rectale* as a validly published homotypic synonym. The record identifies *A. rectalis* as the nomenclatural type of the genus *Agathobacter*.

## Editorial Use

This record supports WikiBiome's selected current name. WikiBiome separately retains the primary objection to the transfer so that a nomenclatural selection is not presented as unanimous phylogenetic agreement.
