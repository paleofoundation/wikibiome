# Takashima and Sugita 2022 — Nakaseomyces glabratus transfer

## Study Scope

Takashima and Sugita reviewed pathogenic-yeast nomenclature and proposed transferring six former *Candida* species into *Nakaseomyces*. They established *Nakaseomyces glabratus* (H.W. Anderson) Sugita & Takashima as a new combination, MycoBank MB 843854, with *Candida glabrata* and *Cryptococcus glabratus* listed in its homotypic synonymy.

## Editorial Use

This is the primary authority for the current combination. It supports retiring *Candida glabrata* as a canonical title while preserving that name when cited literature used it.
