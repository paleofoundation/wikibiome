# NCBI Taxonomy — Akkermansia muciniphila (NCBITaxon:239935)

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 239935 lists *Akkermansia muciniphila* Derrien et al. 2004 as the current species name and identifies Muc, ATCC BAA-835, CIP 107961, and DSM 22959 as type-strain deposits.
