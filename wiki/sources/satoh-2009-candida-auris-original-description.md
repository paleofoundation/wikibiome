# Satoh 2009 — Original description of Candida auris

## Study Scope

Satoh and colleagues described *Candida auris* from a yeast isolated from a hospital patient's external ear canal. They designated JCM 15448 as the type strain and recorded CBS 10913 and DSM 21092 as equivalent deposits.

## Editorial Use

The paper is the primary description of the basionym now combined as *Candidozyma auris*. Its original name is retained when describing the 2009 nomenclatural act or literature that used that name.
