# NCBI Taxonomy — Lancefieldella parvula (NCBITaxon:1382)

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 1382 lists *Lancefieldella parvula* (Weinberg et al. 1937) Nouioui et al. 2018 as the current name and *Atopobium parvulum* as a homotypic synonym.
