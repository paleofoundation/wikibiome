# LPSN — Barnesiella

## Record Scope

LPSN lists *Barnesiella* Sakamoto et al. 2007 as a validly published correct genus name and *Barnesiella viscericola* as its type species.

## Editorial Use

This nomenclatural record supports the accepted bacterial genus spelling. It does not recognize “Barnesella” as a bacterial name or synonym.
