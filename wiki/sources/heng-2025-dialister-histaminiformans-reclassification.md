# Heng 2025 — Dialister histaminiformans reclassification

## Study Scope

Heng and colleagues included a taxonomic revision of Veillonellaceae and proposed the new combination *Dialister histaminiformans* for the species previously named *Allisonella histaminiformans*.

## Editorial Use

This paper supports the current generic placement. The original species description remains the source for the isolate's experimentally observed phenotype.
