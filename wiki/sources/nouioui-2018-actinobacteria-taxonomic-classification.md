# Nouioui 2018 — genome-based Actinobacteria classification

## Study Scope

Nouioui and colleagues used genome-scale phylogenetic analyses to revise the classification of Actinobacteria. Among the resulting nomenclatural changes, they described the genera *Lancefieldella* and *Fannyhessea*, with *Lancefieldella parvula* and *Fannyhessea vaginae* as their respective type species.

## Editorial Use

This is the primary reclassification source for the current combinations [[lancefieldella-parvula]] and [[fannyhessea-vaginae]].
