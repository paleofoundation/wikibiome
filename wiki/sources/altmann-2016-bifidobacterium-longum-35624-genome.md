# Altmann 2016 — Genome analysis of Bifidobacterium longum 35624

## Study Scope

Altmann and colleagues sequenced strain 35624 and compared its core genome with other *Bifidobacterium longum* strains. Their phylogenetic analysis assigned 35624 to *B. longum* subsp. *longum*, correcting its former description as *B. longum* subsp. *infantis* or “*B. infantis* 35624.”

## Editorial Use

Clinical findings published under the historical 35624 label remain attributable to that strain. They must not be presented as subspecies-specific evidence for *B. longum* subsp. *infantis* or generalized to NCBITaxon:1682.
