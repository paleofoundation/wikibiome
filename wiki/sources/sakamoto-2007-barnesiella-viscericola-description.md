# Sakamoto 2007 — Barnesiella viscericola

## Study Scope

Sakamoto and colleagues characterized two Gram-negative, obligately anaerobic, non-spore-forming, non-motile strains isolated from chicken caecum. Phenotypic, chemotaxonomic, and 16S rRNA analyses supported the new genus and species *Barnesiella viscericola*; strain C46 was designated the type strain and deposited as JCM 13660 and DSM 18177.

## Editorial Use

This is the primary description supporting the spelling and establishment of the bacterial genus *Barnesiella*.
