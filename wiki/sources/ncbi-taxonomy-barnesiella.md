# NCBI Taxonomy — Barnesiella (NCBITaxon:397864)

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 397864 lists *Barnesiella* as a bacterial genus in the family Barnesiellaceae and includes *Barnesiella intestinihominis*, *Barnesiella propionica*, and *Barnesiella viscericola* among its children.

## Editorial Use

This database record supports the current genus name and identifier. It does not list “Barnesella” as a bacterial genus or synonym, and it does not resolve the sequence assignment behind that spelling in the Endobiota paper.
