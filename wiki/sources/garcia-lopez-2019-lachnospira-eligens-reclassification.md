# García-López et al. 2019 — Type-Strain Phylogenomics

## Taxonomic Finding

García-López and colleagues analyzed more than 1,000 type-strain genomes and proposed transferring the misplaced *Eubacterium eligens* to *Lachnospira* as *Lachnospira eligens* comb. nov. The proposal was based on genome-scale phylogenetic placement rather than an assumption drawn from later human microbiome associations.

## Nomenclatural Status

The paper was the effective publication for the combination; LPSN records subsequent valid publication in Validation List No. 193. WikiBiome cites LPSN for that validation status and uses the paper as the primary phylogenomic evidence for the transfer.
