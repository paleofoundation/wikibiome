# Species Fungorum — Cladosporium

## Record Scope

Species Fungorum records *Cladosporium* Link 1816 as an accepted genus under Cladosporiaceae and identifies it with genus record 7681.

## Editorial Use

This current fungal database record supports *Cladosporium* as the canonical genus. The historical *Davidiella* relationship is documented separately from the ICTF-supported primary nomenclatural recommendation.
