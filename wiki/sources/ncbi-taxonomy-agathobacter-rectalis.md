# NCBI Taxonomy — *Agathobacter rectalis*

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 39491 lists *Agathobacter rectalis* as the current species name and *Eubacterium rectale* as a homotypic synonym. The record retains comments and references describing the type-strain history and the published dispute over the transfer.

## Editorial Use

WikiBiome uses NCBITaxon:39491 as the stable controlled identifier. The record supports a single current canonical route but does not erase the historical combination or the scientific disagreement documented in the primary literature.
