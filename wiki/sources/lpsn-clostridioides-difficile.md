# LPSN — Clostridioides difficile

## Record Scope

LPSN lists *Clostridioides difficile* (Hall and O'Toole 1935) Lawson et al. 2016 as a validly published combination under the ICNP, the correct name, and the name explicitly recommended for medical use. The page identifies NCTC 11209 and equivalent deposits as type strains.

## Synonym Boundary

LPSN records *Clostridium difficile* (Hall and O'Toole 1935) Prévot 1938 (Approved Lists 1980) as a validly published homotypic synonym. That relationship supports one canonical species record while preserving the older combination in source-era contexts.
