# LPSN — Akkermansia muciniphila

## Record Scope

LPSN lists *Akkermansia muciniphila* Derrien et al. 2004 as a validly published correct name and the type species of *Akkermansia*.

## Editorial Use

This nomenclatural record supports the accepted spelling and current status. It does not recognize “Akkermansia mucinicola” as the name of this species.
