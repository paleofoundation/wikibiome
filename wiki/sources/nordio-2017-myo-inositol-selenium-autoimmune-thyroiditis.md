# Treatment with Myo-Inositol and Selenium Ensures Euthyroidism in Patients with Autoimmune Thyroiditis

## Key Findings

- **TSH decreased after 6 months of myo-inositol plus selenium (Myo-Ins-Se)** in HT patients with subclinical hypothyroidism: 4.32 plus or minus 0.06 to 3.12 plus or minus 0.09 mIU/L, p <= 0.001, n = 86 (quasi-experimental, single-arm, no control group)
- **Thyroid autoantibodies decreased**: TPOAb 720.67 plus or minus 52.39 to 620.38 plus or minus 50.90 IU/ml (p <= 0.001); TgAb 344.96 plus or minus 23.77 to 288.84 plus or minus 23.35 IU/ml (p <= 0.001) (quasi-experimental)
- **Free hormones rose slightly**: fT3 2.67 plus or minus 0.04 to 2.79 plus or minus 0.03 pg/ml (p <= 0.01); fT4 0.94 plus or minus 0.02 to 1.07 plus or minus 0.02 ng/ml (p <= 0.001) (quasi-experimental)
- **Local neck symptom score improved**: 4.67 plus or minus 0.09 to 2.37 plus or minus 0.09 (p <= 0.001) (quasi-experimental)
- **Single hyperthyroid case**: TSH rose from 0.14 to 1.02 mIU/L after 6 months (case-report level observation within the study)

## Methods

- **Design**: Open, single-arm, before-and-after clinical study. No randomization, no control or placebo group, no blinding. The manifest guess "clinical-study (non-randomized)" is consistent with this; assigned quasi-experimental per repo convention.
- **Setting**: Department of Experimental Medicine, University Sapienza, Rome, Italy (author affiliation; recruitment site and dates not otherwise stated).
- **Population**: 87 patients (8 men, 79 women), age range 19 to 65 years, reported mean/median age 42.30 plus or minus 0.06 years. 86 met inclusion criteria: TSH 3 to 6 mIU/L, elevated TPOAb and/or TgAb, normal fT4 and fT3 (i.e. HT with subclinical hypothyroidism). One woman with hyperthyroidism (TSH about 0.14 mIU/L) was also enrolled as a single case. No trace-element, vitamin, antidepressant, or antipsychotic co-treatment. Levothyroxine use is not mentioned.
- **Intervention**: tablets of 600 mg myo-inositol plus 83 ug selenium as L-selenomethionine (Tiroxil, Lo.Li. Pharma Srl, Rome) orally for 6 months, about 2 h before or after meals. Daily number of tablets is not stated.
- **Outcomes**: primary, serum TSH; secondary, TPOAb, TgAb, fT3, fT4, and a 7-question subjective symptomatology (SS) questionnaire on local neck symptoms. Measured at baseline and 6 months.
- **Assays**: enzyme immunometric assay for TSH, fT3, fT4; enzyme luminescence assay for TPOAb and TgAb (Byk-Sangtec).
- **Statistics**: paired t-tests (GraphPad); values reported as "median (plus or minus SEM)"; p <= 0.05 significant.

- The hyperthyroid patient's TSH rose from 0.14 to 1.02 mIU/L.
- No adverse events are reported in results; the conclusion asserts "absence of side effects" without describing safety monitoring.
- The authors propose that myo-inositol improves TSH sensitivity via the inositol-dependent (phospholipase C, Ca2+/diacylglycerol, H2O2) TSH signaling pathway and that selenium acts via selenoproteins (glutathione peroxidases, deiodinases); these mechanisms were not measured.
- The authors state that, unlike selenium alone, the combination lowers TgAb as well as TPOAb; this comparison is not tested within the study.
- No funding statement is printed; authors declare no conflict of interests. The product is a named commercial formulation.

### Limitations (identified from the paper; the authors do not list limitations)

- No control group: regression to the mean (patients selected for elevated TSH 3 to 6), natural fluctuation, and placebo effects on symptoms cannot be excluded.
- Unvalidated 7-item symptom questionnaire; unblinded.
- Selenium status, iodine status, and levothyroxine use not reported.
- Mean post-treatment TSH (3.12 mIU/L) remains above the 2.5 or 3.0 mIU/L upper limits the authors themselves cite as proposed, so "ensures euthyroidism" is not directly demonstrated at the group level; the proportion reaching a normal TSH is not reported.
- Unusual dispersion reporting ("median plus or minus SEM"; age 42.30 plus or minus 0.06) with paired t-tests.

## Relevance to WikiBiome

- Adds low-grade intervention evidence for [[selenium]] combined with myo-inositol in [[hashimotos-thyroiditis]].
- Relevant to [[thyroid-autoimmunity]] (antibody outcomes) but with no control comparison; no microbiome data.

## Relevance to Cureva

- Hypothesis only: myo-inositol 600 mg plus selenomethionine 83 ug daily may lower TSH and antibodies in subclinical HT. Needs randomized, placebo-controlled confirmation before being treated as an effect estimate.
- Selenium dose (83 ug/day) is lower than the 200 ug/day used in most selenium RCTs (see [[huwiler-2024-selenium-supplementation-hashimoto-meta-analysis]]).

## Contradictions / Tensions

- Title and conclusion claim the combination "ensures euthyroidism," which an uncontrolled pre-post design cannot establish.
- Huwiler 2024 meta-analysis found selenium's TSH effect in untreated HT is small (SMD -0.21); the 1.2 mIU/L drop here, without a control arm, may include regression to the mean.

## Open Questions

- What is the effect of Myo-Ins-Se vs selenium alone vs placebo in a randomized, blinded trial?
- What proportion of patients achieved TSH within the reference range, and was it sustained after stopping?
- Did baseline selenium or iodine status modify response?
- Would myo-inositol affect gut microbial inositol metabolism relevant to the gut-thyroid axis?
