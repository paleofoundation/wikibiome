## Study summary

This case-control study combined immune phenotyping, 16S rRNA sequencing, and targeted metabolomics to examine how gut dysbiosis relates to immune dysregulation in Graves' disease. It provides direct human evidence linking microbial and metabolite changes with thyroid autoantibodies, lymphocyte status, and cytokine production.
