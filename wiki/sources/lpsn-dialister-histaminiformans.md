# LPSN — Dialister histaminiformans

## Record Scope

LPSN lists *Dialister histaminiformans* (Garner et al. 2003) Heng et al. 2025 as the validly published correct name. It records *Allisonella histaminiformans* as a validly published homotypic synonym and basionym.

## Editorial Use

This nomenclatural record supports current-name and synonym reconciliation. It does not establish that historical genus-level human microbiome results identify this species.
