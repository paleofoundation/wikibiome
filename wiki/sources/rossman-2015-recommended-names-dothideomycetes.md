# Rossman et al. 2015 — Recommended Names for Pleomorphic Genera in Dothideomycetes

## Record Scope

This peer-reviewed recommendation was produced by the Working Group on Dothideomycetes established under the auspices of the International Commission on the Taxonomy of Fungi. It applies one-fungus/one-name principles to competing generic names.

## Cladosporium and Davidiella

The working group recommends using *Cladosporium* Link 1816 rather than *Davidiella* Crous & U. Braun 2003. It explains that the sexual morph of the *Cladosporium* type species was described as *Davidiella tassiana*, making the generic names synonyms, and selects *Cladosporium* because it has priority, wider use, and substantially more named species.
