# Th17/Treg Cells Imbalance and GITRL Profile in Patients with Hashimoto's Thyroiditis

## Key Findings

- **The Th17/Treg ratio was higher in HT patients than in healthy controls** (p<0.01, figure only) and correlated positively with anti-thyroglobulin antibody (TgAb) levels in HT (r=0.5243, p=0.0176) (case-control)
- **Circulating Treg cells (CD4+CD25+CD127low) were reduced** in HT (p<0.01) with lower PBMC Foxp3 mRNA (p<0.05); the **increase in Th17 cells (CD3+CD8-IL-17+) was only borderline** (p=0.056), while PBMC ROR-γt mRNA was higher (p<0.01) (case-control)
- **Serum IL-6 (p<0.001) and IL-23 (p<0.01) were higher in HT** than controls (case-control)
- **Serum GITRL was higher in HT** (p<0.05) and correlated with Th17 percentage (r=0.5282, p=0.0167) but not with Treg percentage (r=-0.2030, p=0.3907) (case-control)
- **In thyroid tissue, IL-17, ROR-γt and GITRL mRNA were higher** in 6 HT glands than in simple goiter tissue (each p<0.05) (case-control, surgical tissue)

## Methods

- **Design**: Case-control, single centre (Affiliated People's Hospital of Jiangsu University, Zhenjiang, China)
- **Population**: 25 HT patients (M/F 5/20, age 47.2±9.9 y; TgAb 677.1±312.3 IU/mL, TPO-Ab 317.5±206.7 IU/mL), described as "on the period of onset" of HT; 20 healthy controls (M/F 3/17, 46.3±7.2 y). Diagnosis "based on commonly accepted classification criteria". Treatment status (levothyroxine) not reported. Thyroid tissue from 6 HT patients vs simple goiter patients (number of goiter specimens not stated).
- **Measurements**: PBMC flow cytometry (Th17 after 5 h PMA/ionomycin stimulation, gated CD3+CD8- because stimulation downregulates CD4; Treg on unstimulated cells); qRT-PCR for IL-17, ROR-γt, Foxp3 (β-actin reference); serum IL-6, IL-23, GITRL by ELISA; TgAb and TPO-Ab by chemiluminescence.
- **Analysis**: Student's unpaired t-test; Spearman correlation; GraphPad Prism 5. No adjustment for confounders, no multiple-comparison correction reported.

## Detailed Summary

- Most group comparisons are presented only as figures with significance stars; group means for Th17 %, Treg %, IL-6, IL-23 and GITRL are not given in text or tables.
- Representative dot plots show Treg 2.78% (HT) vs 5.73% (control); these are single illustrative samples, not group means.
- Th17 % in PBMCs: increased in HT at p=0.056 (not significant at α=0.05).
- ROR-γt mRNA (PBMC): higher in HT, p<0.01. IL-6: p<0.001. IL-23: p<0.01.
- Treg %: lower in HT, p<0.01. Foxp3 mRNA: lower, p<0.05.
- Th17/Treg ratio: higher in HT, p<0.01; vs TgAb r=0.5243, p=0.0176 (n not stated; correlation with TPO-Ab not reported).
- GITRL serum: higher in HT, p<0.05; vs Th17 % r=0.5282, p=0.0167; vs Treg % r=-0.2030, p=0.3907.
- Thyroid tissue (HT n=6 vs simple goiter): IL-17, ROR-γt and GITRL mRNA all higher, p<0.05.
- Authors conclude increased GITRL "may" drive Th17 expansion; this causal direction is not tested in the study (the supporting GITRL to Th17 mechanism is cited from their earlier collagen-induced arthritis mouse work, not performed here).
- Funding: National Natural Science Foundation of China and Jiangsu provincial grants; no conflicts declared.

## Relevance to Cureva

- Th17/Treg ratio and IL-6/IL-23 are candidate immune readouts for intervention studies in HT, but this paper offers no intervention data.
- GITRL is a speculative target; no human evidence here beyond correlation.

## Contradictions / Tensions

- Th17 elevation in peripheral blood did not reach significance (p=0.056), yet the abstract and discussion describe Th17 as increased.
- Small sample (25 vs 20), no reported treatment status, no adjustment for age/sex, figure-only reporting of most results.
- Simple goiter is not a healthy-tissue comparator.

## Open Questions

- Does the Th17/Treg imbalance in HT relate to gut microbiome composition or intestinal permeability markers (e.g. [[zonulin]])?
- Is GITRL elevation a cause or consequence of thyroid inflammation?
- Does the Th17/Treg ratio change with levothyroxine or selenium treatment?
