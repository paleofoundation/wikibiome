# Mattarelli 2008 — Bifidobacterium longum subspecies reclassification

## Study Scope

Mattarelli and colleagues reviewed genotypic and phenotypic evidence distinguishing the former *B. longum*, *B. infantis*, and *B. suis* groupings and proposed three subspecies of *Bifidobacterium longum*. The paper established *Bifidobacterium longum* subsp. *infantis* comb. nov. and designated strain S12 (ATCC 15697; DSM 20088) as its type strain.

## Editorial Use

This is the primary nomenclatural authority for the current combination. It supports retiring the obsolete species combination as a canonical title while retaining it as a historical name in source-specific contexts.
