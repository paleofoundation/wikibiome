# Anderson 1917 — Original description of Cryptococcus glabratus

## Study Scope

Anderson's study of yeast-like fungi in the human intestinal tract introduced *Cryptococcus glabratus*. The Journal of Infectious Diseases article begins at page 341; fungal nomenclatural records cite the name at page 379.

## Editorial Use

This source documents the basionym and original human-intestinal context. The current-name and type-material relationships are taken from the later nomenclatural sources rather than inferred from the 1917 morphology.
