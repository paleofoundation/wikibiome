# Liu 2024 — Candidozyma auris reclassification

## Study Scope

Liu and colleagues used phylogenomic and comparative-genomic analyses to delimit the *Candida auris-Candida haemuli* clade within Metschnikowiaceae. They proposed the genus *Candidozyma* and established *Candidozyma auris* (Satoh & Makimura) Q.M. Wang, Yurkov, Boekhout & F.Y. Bai as a new combination, MycoBank MB 848169.

## Editorial Use

This is the primary authority for the current combination. The paper explicitly identifies *Candida auris* Satoh & Makimura 2009 as its basionym; it does not describe a second species.
