# NCBI Taxonomy — *Lachnospira eligens*

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 39485 lists *Lachnospira eligens* as the current species name and *Eubacterium eligens* Holdeman and Moore 1974 as its basionym. The record associates the combination with type strain ATCC 27750 = DSM 3376.

## Editorial Use

WikiBiome uses this record for the stable NCBITaxon identifier and current-name relationship. NCBI notes that its taxonomy is curated rather than a governing nomenclatural authority, so WikiBiome also cites LPSN and the effective reclassification publication.
