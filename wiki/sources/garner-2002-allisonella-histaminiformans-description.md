# Garner 2002 — Allisonella histaminiformans description

## Study Scope

Garner, Flint, and Russell enriched histamine-producing bacteria from bovine rumen contents using histidine as an energy source. They proposed the genus and species *Allisonella histaminiformans* for the isolates, including type strain MR2.

## Evidence Boundary

The study demonstrates histidine utilization and histamine production in isolates obtained from a bovine-rumen experimental system. It does not establish human obesity, mast-cell activation, allergic disease, metal dependence, or therapeutic claims.

## Current Name

The species is now classified as [[dialister-histaminiformans]].
