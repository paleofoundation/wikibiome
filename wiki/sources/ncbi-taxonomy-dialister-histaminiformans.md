# NCBI Taxonomy — Dialister histaminiformans (NCBITaxon:209880)

## Record Scope

NCBI Taxonomy record 209880 lists *Dialister histaminiformans* (Garner et al. 2003) Heng et al. 2025 as the current name and *Allisonella histaminiformans* as its basionym. The record places the species in Bacillota, Negativicutes, Veillonellales, Veillonellaceae, and *Dialister*.

## Editorial Use

This record supports current-name, lineage, identifier, synonym, and type-strain reconciliation. It does not support human disease or treatment claims.
