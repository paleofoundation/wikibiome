# Barnesiella

*Barnesiella* is a validly published genus of Gram-negative, obligately anaerobic bacteria in the phylum Bacteroidota and family Barnesiellaceae. NCBI Taxonomy assigns the genus NCBITaxon:397864, and LPSN lists *Barnesiella* Sakamoto et al. 2007 as the correct name [[ncbi-taxonomy-barnesiella]] [[lpsn-barnesiella]]. Sakamoto and colleagues established the genus with *Barnesiella viscericola*, isolated from chicken caecum, as its type species [[sakamoto-2007-barnesiella-viscericola-description]].

## Endobiota Source-Label Caveat

Ata and colleagues reported that a genus printed as “Barnesella” was significantly decreased in stool from 14 women with stage 3/4 endometriosis compared with 14 controls. The study used V3–V4 16S rRNA sequencing and RDP classifier 2.12, and the printed label appears in its results text and table [[ata-2019-endobiota-study-vaginal-cervical-gut-microbiota-endometriosis]]. Colonetti and colleagues later summarized the Ata result under the recognized spelling *Barnesiella* [[colonetti-2023-gut-vaginal-microbiota-endometriosis-systematic-review]].

WikiBiome therefore treats “Barnesella” as a source-transcription or source-label alias for routing and traceability, not as a valid bacterial name or nomenclatural synonym. The original spelling remains visible when the 2019 study or reviews that repeat its wording are described [[miyashira-2022-microbiome-and-endometriosis]] [[ser-2023-current-updates-microbiome-endometriosis-review]]. The later spelling normalization does not by itself prove that the underlying sequence assignment was definitively resolved to *Barnesiella*; that would require reanalysis of the study data against an identified current reference taxonomy.

## Evidence Boundary

The cited Endobiota observation is genus-level, stool-specific, and derived from a small observational cohort. It does not establish causality, a species-level assignment, a vaginal or cervical *Barnesiella* signal, or a functional colonization-resistance mechanism.
