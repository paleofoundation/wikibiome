# Lancefieldella parvula

*Lancefieldella parvula* is the current name for NCBITaxon:1382. *Atopobium parvulum* is a validly published historical homotypic synonym rather than the current canonical name [[ncbi-taxonomy-lancefieldella-parvula]] [[lpsn-lancefieldella-parvula]].

## Taxonomy and Characteristics

Nouioui and colleagues transferred the species to the newly described genus *Lancefieldella* in a genome-based revision of Actinobacteria [[nouioui-2018-actinobacteria-taxonomic-classification]]. Their description characterizes members of the genus as Gram-stain-positive, obligately anaerobic, non-motile, non-spore-forming small rods. LPSN lists *L. parvula* as the type species of the genus and records *Atopobium parvulum*, *Streptococcus parvulus*, and *Peptostreptococcus parvulus* as homotypic synonyms [[lpsn-lancefieldella-parvula]].

## Human Isolation Evidence

The sequenced type strain, IPP 1246 (DSM 20469), was described under the then-current name *Atopobium parvulum*. That genome report notes frequent isolation from the human oral cavity and an association with oral malodor, while distinguishing association from demonstrated causation [[copeland-2009-atopobium-parvulum-genome]]. The current evidence cited here does not support the former page's claims of a specific iron requirement, cysteine-desulfhydrase-driven hydrogen-sulfide injury, oral-to-gut translocation, inflammatory bowel disease, or colorectal-cancer causation.

## Literature Naming

Studies published before or without adoption of the 2018 reclassification may accurately report *Atopobium parvulum*. WikiBiome preserves that historical display name when summarizing such studies, but the link resolves to this single current-name record.
