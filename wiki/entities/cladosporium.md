# *Cladosporium*

*Cladosporium* Link 1816 is the selected current genus name for NCBITaxon:5498 and is accepted as a genus by Species Fungorum [[ncbi-taxonomy-cladosporium]] [[species-fungorum-cladosporium]]. *Davidiella* Crous & U. Braun 2003 was introduced for the sexual morph of *Cladosporium* sensu stricto. Under one-fungus/one-name treatment, the International Commission on the Taxonomy of Fungi working group recommended the older and more widely used *Cladosporium* over *Davidiella*; the latter is therefore retained here only as a historical synonym, not as a second canonical genus [[rossman-2015-recommended-names-dothideomycetes]].

## Nomenclature and Source-Label Boundary

The nomenclatural recommendation connects the type species through the sexual morph: *Davidiella tassiana* was described for the sexual state of *Cladosporium herbarum*, making the generic names synonyms for current editorial purposes [[rossman-2015-recommended-names-dothideomycetes]]. Historical articles may nevertheless print “Davidiella,” and WikiBiome preserves that wording when reporting what a source measured. Canonicalization does not retroactively establish a species-level assignment for an older amplicon label.

Al Bataineh and colleagues reported “Unclassified Davidiella” among the fungal labels increased in 25 adults with type 2 diabetes compared with 25 controls in their Emirati pilot cohort [[al-bataineh-2020-gut-microbiome-fungal-community-t2d-emirati]]. That source-era label is linked to the current *Cladosporium* record for traceability, but the study's unmatched age, body-mass index, diet, and treatment context precludes a causal conclusion, and the unclassified result should not be read as a resolved species identification.

## Reported Mycobiome Contexts

Other cited surveys and reviews use the current name *Cladosporium* in oral, gut, and broader human mycobiome contexts, including hypertension and chronic kidney disease, multiple sclerosis, rheumatoid arthritis, cancer, schizophrenia, and neurological-disease literature [[zou-2022-mycobiome-dysbiosis-hypertension-light-chains]] [[qiu-2023-gut-mycobiome-hypertension-ckd]] [[otaegui-chivite-2025-mycobiota-ms-progression]] [[dagar-2023-bacteriome-mycobiome-virome-rheumatoid-arthritis]] [[ding-2025-mycobiome-human-cancer-mechanisms-therapeutics]] [[wei-2022-oral-mycobiota-pancreatic-adenocarcinoma]] [[ling-2025-gut-mycobiota-schizophrenia-metabolic-syndrome-trichosporon]] [[forbes-2019-fungal-mycobiome-neurological-disease]]. These records support reported detection or differential abundance in their stated cohorts; they do not by themselves establish persistent colonization, causation, pathogenicity, or a genus-wide metal requirement.
