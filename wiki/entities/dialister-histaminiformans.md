# Dialister histaminiformans

*Dialister histaminiformans* is the current name for the species originally described as *Allisonella histaminiformans*. NCBI Taxonomy assigns the species NCBITaxon:209880, and LPSN treats *A. histaminiformans* as its validly published homotypic synonym and basionym [[ncbi-taxonomy-dialister-histaminiformans]] [[lpsn-dialister-histaminiformans]]. The unsupported species epithet used by the retired WikiBiome page is not retained as a taxon or synonym.

## Nomenclatural History

Garner, Flint, and Russell described *Allisonella histaminiformans* from histidine-enrichment cultures of bovine rumen contents. The type strain is MR2, also deposited as ATCC BAA-610, CCUG 48567, and DSM 15230 [[garner-2002-allisonella-histaminiformans-description]]. Heng and colleagues transferred the species to *Dialister* in 2025; NCBI and LPSN now apply the combination *Dialister histaminiformans* [[heng-2025-dialister-histaminiformans-reclassification]] [[ncbi-taxonomy-dialister-histaminiformans]] [[lpsn-dialister-histaminiformans]]. It belongs to the family Veillonellaceae in the phylum Bacillota, not to Bacteroidetes.

## Evidence Boundary

The original isolates were strictly anaerobic bacteria that used histidine as an energy source and produced histamine in the bovine-rumen experimental system [[garner-2002-allisonella-histaminiformans-description]]. That evidence does not establish enrichment in human obesity, metabolic syndrome, mast-cell activation syndrome, or allergic disease, and it does not establish iron-dependent expansion or a therapeutic target. Human cohort results reported under the historical genus label *Allisonella* should remain attributed to that study label unless their sequence data support species-level assignment.

## Literature Naming

Older studies and databases may use *Allisonella histaminiformans* or the genus label *Allisonella*. Those names should be retained when accurately describing the terminology used by a source, while links and current taxonomic statements resolve to this record. Genus-level findings must not be silently rewritten as findings about *D. histaminiformans*.
